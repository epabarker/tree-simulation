# 0023: Adopt the fitted species, with hand adjustments

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Spec:** §9 (species character); ADR 0016 (species per tree), 0020 (fitting), 0021 (hyperbolic discount), 0022 (juvenile phase)

## Context
- **M27 round 2** ([note](../experiments/2026-10-28-m27-fits.md)) fitted all ten species to literature traits. Every measured target is within one band:
  - heights and both growth curves (yield tables);
  - H/D;
  - floor light and the four measured survival lights (Hagemeier & Leuschner 2019).
  - Only described, half-weight crown traits remain off.
- **The hold-out found three faults:**
  - holly too intolerant (8.5% against 2–5%);
  - rowan and pine too tolerant;
  - the mixed wood losing holly, rowan and willow by season 400 under the fitted beech's darker, measured floor.
  - Fitted pine also grew past its range after season 100, which the fit never saw.
- **The user asked** for the rest to be fitted by hand and the fits put on the site (2026-10-28).

## Decision
- **`FLORA` is now the fitted species** (`flora-fitted.ts`), generated by `analysis/experiments/m27_adopt.py` from the round-2 fits and `analysis/fit/hand-overrides.toml`, where each override gives its reason.
  - The hand-tuned species stay in `HAND_FLORA` as a record, and as the subject of two mechanism tests: R2's acrotony and R3's gravitropic recovery.
- **Each patch carries** `r1.hydraulic: "hyperbolic"` and its own `r2.hJ`. The defaults stay off, so presets without species are unchanged and so are the golden hashes.
- **Hand adjustments** (measured with the hold-out's tests; 12 seeds for the mixed wood):

  | Species | Override | Why |
  |---|---|---|
  | Holly | Q_ss 0.013, m_f 0.025, Q_est 0.01, h_j 20 | The fitted holly (Q_ss 0.059, m_f 0.049, h_j 39) starved under the fitted beech and hornbeam (floors 1.3–1.5%) and vanished from the wood. Evergreen leaves are cheap each season; seedlings establish in deep shade (Arrieta et al. 2005) |
  | Rowan | Q_ss 0.06 | M25's value: back in its survival band (8.5%) |
  | Pine | Q_ss 0.2, L_h 30 | Survival 27% (band 20–30%); 27.5 m at 100 (table 29 m), 34.5 m at 150, 37.4 m at 200 |

- **Tests follow the adopted species,** with expectations from measured values:
  - fitted oak saplings need about 13% light, as Hagemeier & Leuschner's 11.4% says, where the hand-tuned oak lived at 8%;
  - holly now outlives hornbeam at 2%, a small inversion of the score order, kept so holly persists under beech.

## Consequences
- **The home forest changes** (garden 0.14.0):
  - heights follow yield tables, so the canopy is taller;
  - floors under beech and hornbeam are darker, as measured;
  - young trees grow slowly, then accelerate.
- **The mixed wood over 400 seasons** (12 seeds, trees ≥ 5 cells a run):
  - through season 200 all eight species are present;
  - by 400 beech (4.7) and hornbeam (2.7) dominate, and oak (0) is gone: the oak-to-beech replacement the M19 note describes;
  - holly (0.3), willow (0.3) and rowan (0.7) persist thinly, against the hand-tuned wood's 2.6, 0.8 and 0.8.
- **Revisit** if a sparser understorey than the hand-tuned wood's reads as wrong on the site, or if measured light under holly, fir or the small trees replaces the estimated floors.
