# 0029: Form by hand, physiology by fit

- **Status:** proposed (drafted 2026-10-31 from the round-5 plan's §4; the user accepts).
- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Amends:** ADR 0020 (fitting species: what is fitted), ADR 0028 (the profile trait leaves the loss and stays reported).
- **Plan and note:** [the round-5 plan](../plans/2026-10-31-species-round5.md); [the species note](../experiments/2026-10-30-species-3d.md), "Round 4: birch, and why the round stopped" and "Round 5".

## Context
Round 3's beech met every measured target as a tiered cone (η 1.18). Round 4's birch met all five of its measured targets as a tiered cone at 60 and a flat-topped pagoda at 100 (`r2.levels.1.setPoint` 83°, κ 1.86). Both are the same move. The loss reads a few shape numbers taken from field-guide prose, at half weight, and the search has nine architectural knobs it can move in any direction the loss does not read. Each round added the observable that would have caught the last failure, and each left free directions behind it.

## Decision
1. **The split.** The ten form parameters are held, set by hand: `r2.levels.0.lambda`, `r2.levels.1.lambda`, `r2.levels.1.setPoint`, `r2.levels.1.postureSetPoint`, `r2.levels.1.branchAngle`, `r2.levels.1.eta`, `r2.levels.2.setPoint`, `r2.aC`, `r2.dLambda`, `r2.kappa`. The posture set point (R3's, ADR 0011) was added on 2026-10-31: the form sweep showed that it, not the growth set point, holds birch's limbs (elevation ≈ 21° across `setPoint` 20–90). A species that leaves it unset has its limbs straighten toward the level's `setPoint`; its `[form]` says so in a comment rather than pinning a number, so that a hand `setPoint` carries the posture with it. They live in each targets file's `[form]` and are applied to every candidate like `[fixed]`. Only the physiology is fitted, by the least-squares and CMA-ES search as built: `c3.alpha`, `c5.Trel`, `c6.r0` (m_w derived), `r1.Lh`, `r1.mf`, `r3.c`, `r6.qMin`, `r6.leafArea`, `r2.hJ`. The Tikhonov term covers those nine; the bounds are unchanged. The form's former bounds stay in the targets file as `[[formRange]]`: the span the form sheet sweeps, never searched.
2. **The loss** keeps the traits a wrong architecture cannot meet once form is held: size, growth, girth, leaf area and light (`stand.height`, `lone.earlyShare`, `stand.lateShare`, `lone.slenderness`, `stand.slenderness`, `stand.lai`, `stand.crownRatio`, `shade.survivalLight`, where the species has them). Weights and kinds are as ADR 0028 left them.
3. **Shape is reported, never scored,** until a shape target is measured. The reported traits move to `[[report]]` and keep their described values as the reference the sheet and the panel are judged against: `lone.profile`, `lone.spread`, `lone.widestAt`, `lone.crownFullness`, `lone.forkHeight`, `lone.leaderDominance`, `lone.branchAngle`, `lone.limbDroop`, and the new `lone.limbElevation` (`form.rs`: the length-weighted median elevation of the first-order limbs' segments, read along the limbs rather than by a chord). Each is reported at 60 and 100. The stand's crown ratio at 100 is reported beside the scored one at 60.
4. **The form sheet records each hand value.** Each species gets a one-knob-at-a-time sweep: the nine knobs other than the posture set point, four values each, the rest at round 2's, natively at seed 1 (the posture set point has its own four-value sweep where a species' limbs need it: birch, oak). A three-knob grid follows on the knobs the user names. The pick goes into `[form]` with a dated comment.
5. **The gallery is the regression set.** `analysis/fit/gallery/<species>-<name>.json` holds every patch the panel rejected, with its verdict and date. The driver measures the LM point and the final point against it (`treesim.gallery`, `m27_gallery.py`). A fit nearer a reject than the hand form is flagged in the results file (`shapeFlag`) and is never accepted silently. Before a round starts, the hand form must be separated from every reject on the reported observables (beyond twice the seeds' noise); where two coincide, the panel decides.
6. **A pilot and its panel come before any overnight run.** `m27_fit.py --pilot` takes one species to the end of LM, draws its line-up panel, and stops. The user judges that panel first.

## Consequences
- Fewer evaluations per round: nine parameters in the screening and the Jacobian instead of eighteen.
- The described shape targets stop being targets and become the brief the sheet is judged against. Shape becomes fitted again by routes C and D (a measured shape target), after shipping.
- The physiology knobs still move shape (α does; λ is held). The step log shows the reported shape at every accepted LM step beside the hand form's, and the gallery check and the panel after LM catch drift.
- Two remaining routes are reported beside the fit rather than closed: `r6.leafArea` meeting LAI with big leaves on a sparse skeleton, and `r3.c` meeting H/D with stress wood at a thin r0.
- Round 3's and round 4's fits remain on file. `m27_rescore.py` now prints their shape under a "reported" heading and no longer scores it.
