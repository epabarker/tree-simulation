# M18: the row-marched sky sweep

## Question
Does a row-marched sweep make the light field more accurate and faster, without changing the stand patterns beyond seed-to-seed noise (§C2.4, §10)?

**How the two sweeps work:**
- **The M6 sweep:** shallow rays step one column at a time and interpolate between two rows at every step, so a ray's optical depth diffuses along its path. On a ring, shallow rays lap the world up to about eight times.
- **The row march:** every ray climbs one row at a time and integrates the exact segment it crosses in each row, using that row's prefix sums of leaf area. It interpolates only once per row, to read the depth on the line above between cell centres.

Expected beforehand:
- **More accurate:** the M6 interpolation averages optical depth, and since exp(−kT) is convex, averaging darkens a patchy canopy.
- **Faster on rings:** there are no laps.
- **Metrics:** within noise.

## Setup
- **Accuracy:** cell light from each sweep against an exact ray trace (steps of 0.01 along each of the same K = 7 rays) on the foliage of `skyStand` (season 30), `forest` (50) and `skyTree` (40), every second cell below the canopy top. Scratch script; the unit test `sky-sweep.test.ts` keeps a small version of it.
- **Cost:** `npm run bench`.
- **Patterns:**
  ```bash
  uv run --project analysis python analysis/experiments/m18_light.py   # 60 runs
  ```
  It runs `skyTree`, `skyStand` and `forest` for 60 seasons, over 10 seeds, with `c2.sweep` = `columns` or `rows`.

## Result

**Accuracy** (mean |ΔQ|/C against the exact trace):

| Preset | Column sweep | Row march |
|---|---|---|
| skyStand | 0.065 | 0.049 |
| forest | 0.061 | 0.048 |
| skyTree | 0.049 | 0.035 |

The row march removes 20–30% of the error. Both sweeps are mirror symmetric to rounding.

**Cost** (light phase, ms a season):

| Case | Before (M17) | Row march |
|---|---|---|
| skyStand | 1.7 | 1.6 |
| forest (ring) | 2.7 | 1.4 |
| wideForest (420 ring) | 5.0 | 2.9 |
| panorama (520 × 140 ring, 55 trees) | — | whole season 7.6 (p95 9.8), against the 30 ms target |

The first version was 10–35% *slower*: it did floors, modulos and side checks per cell. Since a ray's offset is the same for every column of a row, each row is now copied once with padding, and the inner loop only indexes.

**Patterns** (season 60, 10 seeds; change in pooled seed-to-seed SD):

| Preset | Height | H/D | Crown ratio | W/H | Ground light | Metamers | Edge lean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| forest | +1.3 | −5.0 | −0.1 | +0.1 | −0.2 | +1.5 | — |
| skyStand | +4.6 | −6.6 | +0.3 | −1.0 | +1.8 | +1.9 | −1.1 |
| skyTree | +0.4 | −1.9 | −1.0 | +0.2 | +1.1 | +0.4 | — |

- **Within noise:** crown ratio, crown width, edge lean and ground light (|d| ≲ 2).
- **Shifted:** stand trees carry 15–20% more metamers, as more light reaches into the crowns, so their pipe-model trunks are thicker. Stand H/D falls from 75 to 66, still inside 60–80. `skyStand` grows 3 cells taller.
- **Open-grown trees:** 2 of 5 `skyTree` seeds now lose their first leader to a lateral that takes over (a decurrent crown); the main stem carries on upright.
- **Random lean:** every tree's random lean is larger (mean |lean| 1.0 → 1.5 cells on the ring). Edge trees in an open world still lean outward by 2.7 cells, against −0.4 on the ring.

## Tests
- **Goldens:** the 13 sky-model goldens change; the 14 triangle-model ones do not.
- **M6–M7 evidence tests** (`sky.test.ts`, `species.test.ts`) pin the M6 sweep with `M6_LIGHT`, as they already pin `PRE_R1`, so their thresholds keep testing what they measured.
- **Tests of the current model:**
  - **Trunk tilt** (`mechanics.test.ts`) is measured along the main stem (the thickest child at each node), not the order-0 axis, which a decurrent tree turns into a side limb. Posture control holds the stem at 3.7° against 16° without it.
  - **The edge-effect test** (`forest.test.ts`) uses signed outward lean, so the larger random lean averages out.
  - **Carbon:** two bounds widen slightly. The plateau is at 85.1% of the world (bound 85% → 90%), and the starved seedling dies after 21 seasons (bound 20 → 25).
- **New unit tests:**
  - closer to the exact trace than the column sweep;
  - mirror symmetric;
  - one pass round a narrow ring.

## Conclusion
- The row march is the default (`c2.sweep: "rows"`; ADR 0005 addendum).
- It is more accurate, about twice as fast on rings, and the 520-cell panorama costs a quarter of its budget, so the garden's ring may now be up to 520 cells wide.
- The changes it brings are a more accurate canopy's: fuller crowns, slightly thicker trunks, and occasional decurrent open-grown trees.
- **Follow-up:** recalibrate the species (M19–M20) under it, in the realism pass.
