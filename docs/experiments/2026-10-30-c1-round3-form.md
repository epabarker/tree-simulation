# C1 round 3: form, steps 1–2 (girth in 3D, the crown base by leaf area)

_2026-10-30. [The plan](../plans/2026-10-30-form.md) §2–3; [ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-girth-in-3d.md). Code and saved outputs: [`code/2026-10-30-form/`](code/2026-10-30-form/). 3 seeds per variant: exploration, not evidence. Natively, this machine (16 threads)._

## Question
1. With scars recording the pipe radius and r0 ÷ 8, m_w × 64 (k = 8), does stand H/D reach 60–80, the lone tree stay lower, and the height level off?
2. Does a crown base measured by leaf area lift where the branch measure (lowest live first-order branch) cannot?

## Setup
- `rescale.sh`: `forest` and its own lone tree, seeds 1–3, 300 seasons, snapshots 150, 200, 300 with `--light`. `base` (scars fixed, r0 0.02, m_w 1) against `k8` (the new defaults), each at τ 0.2 and τ 0. **The lone tree** is round 2's control: `forest`'s params, one tree on a 210-cell world with open sides and the woods' top (200), O4 off. Every "lone" row below is this generic tree. The first runs used `oakOpen` (the fitted oak, through `--species-patch`), a different model. Its rows are kept as a species reading, marked clipped (`trunk-oak200.txt`, `leaf-oak200.txt`).
- `storms.sh` (after the first stop): `forest` k8 with storms off (`o2.stormP` 0), and with σ_break 9000.
- `sigma-break.sh` (after the second stop): windthrow's ramp from H/D 80 (ADR 0027), σ_break 6000, 7000, 8000.
- `mixed.sh`: `mixedWood` at σ_break 7000, τ 0.2.
- Readers: `trunk.py` (stand, plateau, deaths by cause, the trunk's pipe² split, radii and stress by order), `collapse.py` (heights by season, breakage per storm), `leaf.py` (step 2), `mixed.py`. Outputs: the `.txt` files beside them.

## Result

### Step 1, first reading: storms break the crowns
At k8 with σ_break 4500, stand H/D was 66 at 150 (the plan's scratch test), then **35–40 at 300**: heights fell from 67 to 44 between 150 and 200 and swung between 30 and 75 after. R3 breakage took 20–70k metamers in single seasons. Every breakage season broke 82–95% of the planted trees' metamers, against 0.01–0.9% at the old scale (`collapse.txt`).

**Why:** stress wood now sets every radius. Every metamer carries stress wood, and 25–54% are at σ ≥ σ_target by order (`trunk.txt`). σ_break was 1.5 × σ_target and a storm loads the top 2.5×. At the old scale the branches were pipe-thick, below σ_target, and storms broke nothing. The trunk's pipe² split was 2.6% tips, 97.4% scars and memory, with the children's pipe² 99.9% of the root's. The scars are pipe-sized now, so the trunk is not "scars of mechanics": the H/D loss was height lost to storms.

### Step 1, storms off: the rescale is sound

| `forest` k8, storms off, s300 | H/D | height (250 → 300) | metamers/seed | crown ratio by leaf | floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| τ 0.2 | 77.6 | 81.5 → 82.8 | 67.6k | 0.59 | 14.7% |
| τ 0 | 73.3 | 79.6 → 80.4 | 46.5k | 0.46 | 13.2% |

No breakage, no windthrow (H/D was the only thing missing for it). The plateau is today's (≈ 82), not the scratch test's 67: that drop was storms.

### Step 1, σ_break 9000: no breakage, but windthrow
Storms broke nothing, and windthrow threw **46 of 90** planted trees by 300 (42 at τ 0). Its slenderness factor ramped from H/D 40, so a stand at 78 sat two-thirds of the way to maximum risk. It was dormant in 3D while H/D was 10.

### Step 1, the ramp from H/D 80 and the σ_break sweep (storms on)

| σ_break | per storm (median share broken) | storms > 10% | H/D s300 (τ 0.2 / τ 0) | height 250 → 300 (τ 0.2) | thrown of 90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6000 | 38–61% | all | 57 / 60 | 64.8 → 69.9 | 3 / 2 |
| **7000** | **3.1–6.0%** (worst 10–40%) | 2–8 per seed | **70 / 70** | **77.6 → 77.6** | **2 / 0** |
| 8000 | none | – | 80 / 73 | 82.9 → 85.7 | 6 / 5 |

**Chosen: 7000.** The median storm drops 3–6% of the stand's metamers, the worst 10–40% (2–8 storms per seed over 300 seasons). Storm severity is C5's calibration, not this step's. Under it `forest` at 300 has 68k metamers per seed (τ 0.2) and 50k (τ 0), floor 14.8% and 12.2%, crown width 27–28 at a 21-cell spacing. 51–53 of 90 planted trees are alive, losing 35–36 to O4's background mortality.

**The lone tree** (the generic one, storms on as in `forest`; `trunk-lone.txt`, `lone-heights.txt`): H/D 46–48 at 150 and **40.5 (τ 0.2) and 42.4 (τ 0) at 300**, below the stand's 70. It levels off below the top: median 88.8 and 87.4 at 300. The median gain over 250–300 is −2.2 and +0.1; seed 3 still climbs slowly (+3 and +6 cells). Before the rescale the same tree stood at 83–86 with H/D 6.

**The fitted oak** (`oakOpen`, a species reading, **clipped**): at its old top (120) the crown was cut flat at ≈ 127. With the woods' top (200, now the preset's) it climbs about 1.4 cells a season (55 at 25, 125 at 75, 160 at 100) and is pinned at 201–208 by season 150 in all 6 runs. Its readings (H/D 41–44, leaf crown ratio 0.84–0.86 at 300) describe a clipped tree.

**The mixed wood** at 7000 (3 seeds, 300): windthrow 10 of 90 planted (11%): birch 9 of 27 (thrown at median H/D 128, height 99), oak 1. Beech 6 of 9 alive (H/D 76); hornbeam and oak survivors run to 170–174 (track 3's known issue).

### Step 2: the crown base by leaf area
`leafBase` is the height below which 5% of the live buds' leaf area lies; `leafCrownRatio` = (height − leafBase)/height. Stand (planted median, σ_break 7000) against the generic lone tree, at 300 (`leaf.txt`):

| seed | τ 0.2: stand 150 → 300 | lone | τ 0: stand 150 → 300 | lone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.58 → 0.61 | 0.78 | 0.45 → 0.56 | 0.71 |
| 2 | 0.58 → 0.64 | 0.78 | 0.35 → 0.44 | 0.75 |
| 3 | 0.56 → 0.58 | 0.81 | 0.43 → 0.52 | 0.79 |

(The first reading was against the clipped `oakOpen`, 0.72–0.77; the stop rule is read against the generic tree.)

The branch measure reads 0.98–0.99 throughout. Leaf ≤ branch in every stand row but 3.4% of one seed's (τ 0, seed 1). Those are suppressed trees with no live first-order branch, where the branch measure sets bole = height (ratio 0) while their leaves sit on the trunk.

## What would have stopped it
- **Step 1:** stand H/D at 300 outside 50–100 (it was, at σ_break 4500: the first stop); storms throwing > 20% of planted trees (at σ_break 9000: the second stop); height still climbing over 250–300 (≥ 2 cells) or above 180; the look rejected.
- **Step 2:** the lone tree's `leafCrownRatio` below the stand's at 300 in every seed at τ 0 (the generic lone tree is above in 3 of 3).
- **The generic lone tree** failing to level off (it levels off at 87–89: the rescale did not break the open-grown plateau).

## Outside the plan's expected values (not stops; reported)
- **Stand H/D** falls 82 → 70 from 150 to 300; the plan expected it to keep rising slowly. Trunks thicken while the height holds.
- **Metamers** are 68k per seed at τ 0.2 (expected ≈ 55k), against 105k before.
- **The plateau** is 77–83 (expected ≈ 67): the scratch test's lower plateau was storm breakage.
- Reviewed by the user: H/D falling is expected (radii never shrink), and the scratch test's metamer and plateau figures were taken under storm damage.
- **The stand's leaf crown ratio** rises 150 → 300 at τ 0 (step 4 wants it falling): crowns do not lift yet. This is carried into step 3 (below).

## Follow-ups
- **Step 3's closure analysis** takes the rising leaf crown ratio. With windthrow now 0–2 of 90, it should say whether R1 starvation or O4's background mortality opened the gaps. By 300, 35–36 of 90 planted trees died of "other" causes (O4's background) and 0–3 starved.
- **`flora-fitted.ts` no longer matches what `m27_adopt.py` generates.** The `in3d` overlay in `flora.ts` applies phyllotaxis and E at runtime, and the adopt script emits them into the file. The script must reproduce the current file exactly before track 3 regenerates it. This round edited r0, m_w and σ_break in it by hand; the script carries the same scale.
- **The look's three visible problems are not this step's:**
  - the flat canopy lid over bare lower twigs is the crown base: steps 3–4;
  - the mixed wood's green wall from the ground is the faces at τ 0.2 with undepthed leaves: I3;
  - the clipped oak is the top: track 3.
- **The fitted oak runs to the top** (track 3). With the rescale it climbs about 1.4 cells a season and is pinned at 200 by season 150 in every run: the same class as beech and hornbeam, now with oak. That strengthens the case for the re-fit before the garden ships. Its inverted-triangle crown comes from oak's 2D-fitted R2 rows: left as it is.

## Conclusion
With pipe-radius scars and k = 8, r0 sets H/D again (70–78 in a stand, 40–42 for the lone tree), and the plateau holds for both. Two M16 calibrations made under fat 2D-era pipes had to follow (ADR 0027): σ_break 7000 (a storm drops a few percent of the stand's limbs, not the crown), and windthrow's ramp from §10's H/D 80. The crown ratio by leaf area separates the stand (0.44–0.64) from the lone tree (0.71–0.81) where the branch measure reads 0.99 for both. It does not yet lift over time: that, the floor (12–15%) and canopy closure are steps 3–4.
