## The garden's planting (the user's decision, 2026-10-30)
**The cause of the overlap:** `forestParams` planted round 2's three staggered rows across the depth (`perArea`), so a plate that held 7 trees in 2D held 21 plus recruits, all projected onto one strip.

**The decision:** the picture sets the garden's planting, not the calibration.
- **One row:** the garden plants one row of trees down the slab's middle, as the 2D garden did, each tree a 3D crown.
- **The rest keep per-area planting:** round 2's planting per area stays for the calibration's forest and the woods. It is reversed for `forestParams` only.
- **Recruits follow the row:**
  - they are capped per cell of width, at the ring's density (300 on 210 cells, the 2D garden's `mixedWood` density) times the rows;
  - they establish only in a band of `rows × spacing` cells (21 for one row: about a crown's depth) around the slab's middle;
  - the band is O4's new `band` option, absent everywhere else, so the goldens hold. Local seeds landing outside the band are lost; seed rain lands inside it.
- **For the phone check,** `garden.html` takes `?rows=`, `?spacing=` and `?recruits=` (a factor on the per-width cap).
- **The defaults, from the user's readings** (seed 10, on the phone):
  - spacing 21 looked good, but two trees stood very close (perhaps an early seedling taking off), so it was unclear which was in front, and the plate looked cluttered;
  - recruits ×0.5 looked little different;
  - spacing 28 felt nicer;
  - two rows at spacing 24 looked nice but mildly cluttered.

  So the defaults are one row, spacing 28, recruits ×1: 2 trees on a 66-cell plate, 6 on 160.
- **The closed canopy is not yet judged:** the ambient pace after the intro was too slow to wait for season 100.
- **Step 2's plate cap and frame** stay as built.

## The memory problem (2026-11-02)

STATUS's known issue: 342.5 MB on the 160-cell plate with the specimen at 200 seasons, against ADR 0026's 256 MB. Same branch and machine as step 3.

**The 342.5 MB was the old worker.** That figure is `memory.ts`'s "both live" case, the two worlds side by side, as the worker held them until `claude/foliage` (foliage note §4): its `swap` keeps the forest as checkpoint bytes while the specimen grows. Re-measured at 7edea40 with the species as adopted (foliage's `memory.ts`, one process per case):
- both live: 325.2 MB;
- the worker's swap: 224.0 MB;
- the garden alone: 198.8 MB;
- the specimen alone: 142.0 MB.

That script snapshots every 50 seasons. The worker snapshots every season, and with that the swap is **241.9 MB**: under the budget, by 14 MB.

### Where it goes
`memstats/` builds tree-core under a counting allocator. It is a measuring build, not the product's, made by `memstats.sh`. `memory-split.ts` runs it the way the worker does:
- dead trees are discarded;
- the garden's snapshot is taken every season;
- it reports linear memory, the allocator's live bytes and their peak, and the live bytes by holder (reserved capacity).

The garden at 160 cells, season 200, at 7edea40 (71.8k metamers, 229 trees):

| Holder | Reserved | In use | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light fields (ℓ, Q, V: 5 × f32 a cell) | 41.0 MB | 41.0 | the plate's W × H × D, from season 0 |
| The lanes' accumulator (`compute_lanes`) | 43.2 MB | ≈ 27 | 4 × f32 a cell below the foliage's top, grown by doubling as the top rises |
| Owner grid (O2) | 8.2 MB | 8.2 | i32 a cell |
| Per-tree scratch (`q_slot`, `v_slot`, …) | 32.5 MB | 19.8 | per row, never compacted |
| Metamer tables | 17.8 MB | 12.9 | 179 B a row |
| Bud tables | 11.1 MB | 7.7 | 111 B a row |
| The snapshot's buffer | 8.4 MB | ≈ 6 | rebuilt each season by doubling, beside the last one |
| **Live** | **162.9 MB** | | peak 177.2 MB |
| **Linear memory** | **208.5 MB** | | 31 MB of it the allocator's holes |

- **Memory follows the plate, then the doublings.** Half of it is the plate's grids (91 MB with the accumulator). Linear memory steps up when a large buffer doubles: the realloc holds the old and the new block at once, and the high-water mark keeps both. The garden's 158 → 208.5 MB between seasons 60 and 80 is the accumulator going from 22 to 43 MB.
- **The swap's peak was the checkpoint.** `save` writes the 21 MB checkpoint by doubling (to 32 MB, beside a 16 MB copy) while the forest is still whole: 208.5 → 241.9 MB. The specimen then grows inside what the forest freed.

### The levers, in the brief's order
1. **The specimen's world shares nothing it needs not:** `World.into_checkpoint` (tree-wasm) and `RustWorld.intoCheckpoint`. They take the world and free what a checkpoint does not hold (the light, the owner grid, the per-season scratch, the snapshot's buffers), then write the same bytes into that space. The worker's `swap` uses it.
2. **Capacity the `Vec`s keep, beyond their use:**
   - **the accumulator** is sized once for the whole slab (4 × W × H × D floats, 32.8 MB at 160), not grown with the top (`slab_sweep.rs`);
   - **the snapshot** is written into last season's buffer, reserved for this season's size (an upper bound) with a sixteenth to spare (`snapshot.rs`).

   Not pulled: tighter growth for the tables and the scratch (12.5% slack instead of doubling). It would save about 15 MB more, but it touches `tree.rs`'s table macro and `ensure`, which every principle's scratch goes through, and nothing needs it.
3. **Column types:** not needed, so not asked.
4. **The plate's cap:** stays at 160.

The reused buffer is also a little faster. `snapshot-ab.ts` interleaves the two builds' snapshots of the same 160-cell world at season 100, A then B, median of 15, both orders: 14.2 → 13.6 ms and 17.1 → 16.3 ms. The laptop ran warm after the check, so its times are above step 3's.

Nothing changes what is computed. A SHA-256 of every season's snapshot (garden and lab options) and of the checkpoint, for the garden at 66 and the specimen over 60 seasons, is the same before and after (`same-bytes.ts`). `into_checkpoint`'s bytes are `save`'s, and `checkpoint.test.ts` holds that a world restored from them grows on to the same hash. ## Follow-ups
- **Two trees very close together** at spacing 21. Recruits establish wherever no live trunk is within a cell (O4), so a seedling can take off beside a planted tree. If it recurs at 28, a garden-only minimum distance from a planted trunk is the lever.
- **Judging the garden at season 100** needs a faster way there than the ambient pace (e.g. a dev-page `?intro=` for the intro's length).
- **The per-frame leaf write** (if the phone needs it). Each frame writes every leaf of the layout, hidden ones at size 0, so that the slice table holds for the whole snapshot. Writing only the drawn leaves, with the slice table rebuilt per frame, would save most of step 1's +0.5–0.6 ms a frame.
