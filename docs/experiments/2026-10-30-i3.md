## Step 1: the picture in depth

**What changed:**
- the snapshot's `zOrder` column (rows far to near, sorted in Rust) replaces the page's sort;
- the three `ref*` columns are dropped;
- 64 depth slices for wood and leaves (Canvas 2D: 8);
- leaves are foreshortened by √(Hx² + Hy²) of their shoot;
- ADR 0008's pseudo-depth is removed, and with it the `depth` and `lite` view options and adaptive quality's last ("lite") level.

**Step 1's acceptance (the user's, after the frame bench):**
- **The per-snapshot total is below "before" in every case: yes,** by 55–60%.
- **The Rust snapshot's rise is smaller than the sort it replaces: yes,** 1.3–2.4 ms against 13–31 ms.
- **Draw p50 is up by under 1 ms in the ABAB medians: yes.**
- **The per-frame Scene work is within 0.3 ms of "before": no,** +0.5–0.6 ms. The frame now copies the slice table, clears every leaf's size and writes each leaf to its slice-ordered place, hidden ones included. Not profiled further: the pass was time-boxed to one.

**Accepted as built (the user).** Two criteria were withdrawn:
- **"Falls by at least the JavaScript sort's share",** as too strict. The layout's order changed with the sort's removal (it had to come out far to near), so the sort's time could not simply vanish from the total.
- **The 0.3 ms per-frame bound.** 0.5–0.6 ms is the slices' price; the plan's stop rule (slices over 4 ms a frame) stands and is far from tripped.

## Step 2: the plate, the frame and the recruits

**What changed:**
- `PLATE_HEIGHT` (130) replaces `WORLD_HEIGHT` in `plateWidth` (now in `src/web/plate.ts`);

- **The garden** runs with no visible lag at a phone's and a laptop's plate, and `?bench` was smooth too. The caption's numbers follow if the user can copy them.

## The garden's planting (the user's decision, 2026-10-30)
**The cause of the overlap:** `forestParams` planted round 2's three staggered rows across the depth (`perArea`), so a plate that held 7 trees in 2D held 21 plus recruits, all projected onto one strip.

**The decision:** the picture sets the garden's planting, not the calibration.
- **One row:** the garden plants one row of trees down the slab's middle, as the 2D garden did, each tree a 3D crown.
- **The rest keep per-area planting:** round 2's planting per area stays for the calibration's forest and the woods. It is reversed for `forestParams` only.
- **Recruits follow the row:**
  - they are capped per cell of width, at the ring's density (300 on 210 cells, the 2D garden's `mixedWood` density) times the rows;
  - they establish only in a band of `rows × spacing` cells (21 for one row: about a crown's depth) around the slab's middle;
  - the band is O4's new `band` option, absent everywhere else, so the goldens hold. Local seeds landing outside the band are lost; seed rain lands inside it.
- **For the phone check,** `garden.html` takes `?rows=` (default 1), `?spacing=` (default 21) and `?recruits=` (a factor on the per-width cap, default 1). The defaults are fixed after the user's numbers.
- **Step 2's plate cap and frame** stay as built.

## Follow-ups
- **The per-frame leaf write** (if the phone needs it). Each frame writes every leaf of the layout, hidden ones at size 0, so that the slice table holds for the whole snapshot. Writing only the drawn leaves, with the slice table rebuilt per frame, would save most of step 1's +0.5–0.6 ms a frame.
