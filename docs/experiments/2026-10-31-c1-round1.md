# C1, round 1: what holds the floor up, the crown base down and the crowns narrow

## Question
C1 calibrates the light and form core (C2–C5) in 3D ([review](../reviews/2026-10-29-3d-port-review.md) §4), for an isolated tree and a stand:
- floor light under a closed stand, 2–10% of open sky (§10, M6e);
- a crown base that lifts in a stand and not on an open-grown tree;
- edge lean;
- the interior's τ;
- foliage coverage, with today's leaf drawing (§2e).

The user added three measured targets:
- m (the stand's metamers);
- crown depth;
- individual crown tops (crowns that keep tops of their own, not a mat).

Round 1 asks where each stands at the default, which knob moves it, and what blocks it.

**Order, in writing.** The review and STATUS put I3 (the renderer) before C1. The user asks for C1 now. ADR 0024 allows it: C1 reports coverage with today's leaf drawing, and I3 tunes the drawing against C1's stands. Nothing here waits on I3, but coverage numbers are provisional until I3's leaves are drawn in depth.

## Setup


**The runs:**
- **The stand:** `forest` (10 trees on the 210-cell ring, every principle, O4 recruiting), D = 64, K 16 rotating, the wood interior (τ 0.2), 100 seasons, snapshots at 45 and 100. One knob is changed at a time. The planted trees are measured.
- **The lone tree:** the same rules with one tree in the middle of a 210-cell world with open sides, O4 off.
- **The edge:** the stand with its front face open (τ 1).

**The measures** (`c1.py`), means of three seeds of the planted trees' medians:
- floor: the ground row's light, averaged over the slab's depth;
- crown ratio: 1 − bole/height, the bole being the lowest first-order branch;
- width / H and depth / H: the crown's x and z extents over its height;
- H/D;
- live metamers;
- upper cover: the share of the picture's 1-cell cells holding a leaf (a metamer up to 4 seasons old) in the canopy's upper half;
- own tops: the share of canopy trees whose top leaf is the highest within 4 cells of it;
- lean z: the crown's centroid offset in depth.

**Three seeds.** These are explorations, not evidence. The evidence tests that come out of C1 take 20 seeds (AGENTS.md).

### What holds each target

**1. The floor is held by R6's Q_ss, then by gaps, not by leaf area or τ.**
- **Q_ss sets it:** Q_ss 0.2, 0.1, 0.05 and 0.03 give floors of 23, 15, 12 and 11%. Leafy short shoots stay on down to Q_ss, so the canopy keeps leaf until its lowest leaves reach it: the canopy adjusts its leaf area, as M6e found in 2D.
- **k_ext, τ (0.05–0.35), T_rel, the sky's exponent and three rows of trees barely move it.** τ changes how much leaf the canopy carries (41–83k metamers), not the floor.
- **Below Q_ss ≈ 0.05 the floor levels off near 10%.** In the grown stand at season 45 (`floor.ts`):
  - the floor under the densest tenth of ground columns (LAI 30) is 11%, under bare columns 13–20%;
  - 30–35% of the ground has no leaf above it.

  The light comes in obliquely through the gaps between crowns, not down through them.
- **τ caps the floor's dark end:** in the light model alone, a uniform canopy of any density over the whole slab leaves 6–8% on the floor at τ 0.2, and 0.4% at τ 0.01 (LAI 12). An interior floor below ≈ 6% needs a τ near or below 0.1.

**2. The crown base does not lift: the lowest branches pay their way.**
- The crown ratio is 0.99 in every run, the lone tree's too.
- **The lowest first-order branches are the first ones** (born in seasons 0–3), alive at season 100 with 13–500 metamers each. They are not epicormic: R4 off and q_rel 0.3 change nothing.
- **With R1 on, `forest` sheds by carbon:** a branch goes when its production falls below its maintenance, or its light per metamer below T_rel·Q_top. These branches get 0.10–0.29 of the sky a metamer through the same side gaps, and stay in credit. T_rel 0.2 does not lift the base either.
- **The base lifts only when the lower stand goes dark,** so it waits on closure (3).

**3. The crowns are narrow, and the trees spend their growth on height: R5's own-shade signal.**
- **At the default the trees grow tall and thin:**
  - the stand goes from 74 to 113 cells between seasons 45 and 100 while its metamers fall, and the upper half of the picture goes from 34% to 6% leafy;
  - the lone tree climbs to 122 with a crown 0.27 × height wide, its metamers flat at 10k.
- **R5 sensing only its neighbours' shade** (as the woodland presets already do) changes that:
  - the lone tree's crown is 0.75 × height wide and 0.67 deep at season 45; it is 85 tall at season 100 (122 at the default), with twice the metamers;
  - the stand is 81 tall at season 100 (113 at the default; 2D's plateau was 78), its crowns 0.46 × height deep, the upper canopy a third leafy.

  Two snapshots cannot show a plateau: whether height levels off is C3's to measure.
- **R5 off gives the same:** `forest`'s R5 was reading its own crown's shade as competition. A 3D crown shades its own interior far more than a planar one does.
- **The other knobs:** λ 0.42 widens crowns less (0.41 alone); L_h 30 lowers height but shrinks the crowns with it.

**4. The interior's τ:** τ moves leaf area and m, not the floor's mean (above). It sets the floor's dark limit (1). C1 can choose it only once closure is fixed. Until then 0.2 stays.

**5. m:** the candidate carries 99k metamers in `forest` at season 100, against 59k at the default and ≈ 1.9k in 2D. m rises from ≈ 31 (I2's, at season 100) to ≈ 52. The phone's season scales with it: the passes take ≈ 40% of the season, so ≈ +30% on today's 145 ms for the interior. That would still be under 250 ms, but it has to be measured.

## What would have stopped this
- **The floor result:** if the floor had tracked k_ext or τ, the light model or the faces would be the cause, and the fix would be there. It tracked Q_ss and then levelled off, with no dependence on the leaf area above.
- **The crown result:** if R5 off had left the lone tree a pole, the cause would be allocation (C3, R1). R5 off made it broad.

## Disagreement, in writing
- **R5's own-shade signal should not be `forest`'s and the SKY presets' default in 3D.** 2D calibrated it (M10) on planar crowns. The woodland already uses neighbours.
- **The crown base and the floor are one problem with the canopy's closure.** Calibrating them one at a time (Q_ss alone, T_rel alone) moves numbers without fixing the cause.
- **The 2–10% floor may be the wrong test while a 64-deep slab holds one row of trees.** The stand is a row of crowns with gaps in depth, not a closed stand. Either the planting fills the depth, or the floor is measured under the crowns.

## Conclusion
- **No single C2–C5 knob reaches the targets.** The floor is held up by Q_ss and by gaps. The crown base stays at the ground because the lowest branches pay their way in the side light. The crowns are narrow because R5 reads a crown's own shade.
- **Round 1's candidate:** R5 on neighbours, Q_ss 0.03. It gives:
  - a floor of 10.4% at season 100 (the band's edge);
  - stand height 83 at season 100, the lone tree 90 (113 and 122 at the default);
  - lone crowns 0.52 × height wide;
  - own tops kept;

  and costs about 1.7× the metamers. It is not applied: it changes every golden and leaves the crown base and H/D (13, against 60–80; C2's girth) unsolved.
- **For the user:**
  - accept R5 on neighbours for `forest` and the SKY presets in 3D?
  - should the stand fill the depth (planting) or should the floor be measured under the crowns?
- **Round 2 then:** closure (crown spread against the gaps, with the candidate), the crown base under a closed canopy, then the interior's τ; 20 seeds for whatever becomes evidence.
