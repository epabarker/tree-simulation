# Before C1: the canvas fallback, memory, the evidence harness, threads and the world's top

## Question
The user's checklist before calibration (2026-10-31), after V and the phone's timings:
0. Why the preview's Canvas 2D fallback showed a blank or 43 × 72 canvas, and whether Canvas 2D paints a 3D stand at all.
1. A memory budget: what a world holds, measured, and a rule.
2. The 14 backlog tests that could not run cleanly (9 "harness", 4 out of memory, 1 timeout): make them run, thresholds untouched.
3. The phone's season against the 250 ms budget.
4. What WASM threads would need (nightly Rust), and whether worker lanes are the production path.
5. The world's top: raise it, or open the slab to the sky, costed in light and memory.

## Setup
This branch, Node 24.22.2, the 4-core VM (busy with the evidence suite during items 1 and 4's timings, so their times are upper bounds).
- **0:** `src/web/render/canvas2d.test.ts` (Node, a recording 2D context); the preview's `i2.ts`.
- **1:** `code/2026-10-31-pre-c1/memory.ts <case> 200`, one process per case, so the module's linear memory (which never shrinks) is that case's peak. Cases: the garden's planting (`forestParams`, seed 10) at 66, 160, 320 and 520 cells, with O4's recruits and without (`-planted`); `forest`; the specimen (Plate II's oak); the garden at 160 with the specimen beside it (the worker's `swap` holds both). Each world steps as the worker steps it (discarding the dead), with a full snapshot every 20 seasons. Output: `memory-h130.jsonl` (the top at 130, as it was) and `memory.jsonl` (at 200).
- **2:** `npm run evidence:2d` (output summarised under Result).
- **4:** `code/2026-10-31-pre-c1/lane-return.ts`.
- **5:** `code/2026-10-31-pre-c1/ceiling.sh` (`forest`, interior, top 130 and 200, seeds 1–3, 150 seasons, natively), and the garden's planting at 160 cells with O4 off, top 130 and 200, 200 seasons (`treesim --preset mixedWood --params <forestParams(160, 10) as JSON>`).

## Result

### 0. The Canvas 2D fallback
- **The preview's bug, not the renderer's.** `i2.ts` drew only when the renderer was WebGL2 (through the slab view), so with Canvas 2D nothing was ever drawn after the first resize, and nothing redrew when the canvas changed size. It now draws with either renderer (Canvas 2D as the garden does) and redraws on resize. The preview deploy is unchanged until the user asks for it to be rebuilt.
- **Canvas 2D paints a 3D stand.** The new test grows the garden's planting at 66 cells for 20 seasons (12k metamers), draws it through `Canvas2DRenderer` into a recording context in Node, and counts where the wood strokes and leaf fills land: 43.6k wood points (each segment's two, at three ring offsets), 95% inside the canvas, and wood and leaves in all six vertical bands. Canvas 2D has no depth order (leaves after all wood, by colour and width); that is I3's, as for WebGL2.

### 1. Memory
Peak linear memory of the core, MB, over 200 seasons (the top at 200):

| Case | s0 | s40 | s100 | s200 | Peak | Live metamers, s100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| garden 66 | 22 | 52 | 70 | 70 | **70** | 10k |
| garden 160 | 51 | 123 | 165 | 165 | **165** | 33k |
| garden 160, planted only | 51 | 122 | 164 | 164 | **164** | 26k |
| garden 320 | 101 | 247 | 339 | 339 | **339** | 51k |
| garden 520 | 163 | 400 | 537 | 553 | **553** | 87k |
| `forest` (210) | 66 | 184 | 184 | 239 | **239** | 60k |
| specimen (oak, 160 × 120) | 31 | 85 | 104 | 104 | **104** | 36k |
| garden 160 + specimen | 81 | 198 | 240 | 257 | **257** | 69k |

- **Memory follows the plate's area, not the trees:** about 1 MB a cell of width at D = 64 and a 200-cell top. The planted garden peaks where the recruiting one does with a fifth fewer metamers.
- **At birth a world is ≈ 25 B a cell** (the light field's leaf area, Q and V, 20 B, and the owner grid, 4 B). The lanes' accumulator adds 16 B a cell below the highest leafy row. The peak is ≈ 80 B a cell: the rest is tables, snapshot buffers and the allocator's growth in steps (vectors that double, an accumulator that grows with the top). That ≈ 40 B a cell is not yet accounted for row by row, and is the first place to look for savings.
- **Snapshots:** 1–14 MB a season (0.1 kB a metamer), moved, not copied.
- **Before the top was raised (130):** the garden at 160 reached 1.5 GB by season 200 and the one at 320 ran out of WASM's 4 GB near season 190 (item 5).

**The rule (ADR 0026, proposed):** the garden's worker holds at most **256 MB** of linear memory, every world and lane included, for 200 seasons at the widest plate it serves. Today that holds for a phone's plate (≈ 70 MB at 66) and a laptop's at 160 (165 MB; 257 with the specimen, just over). It does not hold for plates above ≈ 200 cells: the garden serves up to 520 (553 MB). The garden must cap its plate at I3, or the core must cut its bytes a cell. Tools and tests free worlds as they go (`RustWorld.dispose` is now idempotent, so they can free early).

### 2. The evidence harness
**The diagnosis was wrong.** No stand was shared across tests in the files that failed: `world.test.ts` and `flora.test.ts` build their worlds inside each test. What happened:
- **A trap poisons the module.** A world that outgrows WASM's 4 GB traps (`unreachable`) mid-call. wasm-bindgen's borrow flag on that world is never released, so the cleanup's `free()` throws "attempted to take ownership of Rust value while it was borrowed", and later calls into the module fail too.
- **Who traps:**
  - `shadow` (C3 `none` under light) does not regulate in 3D. It doubles every season to 3.6M metamers and traps at season 22;
  - R1 off (an ablation) grows past 1.5M metamers by season 84;
  - per-seed worlds were kept until the test ended (`species.test.ts`'s stands, `flora.test.ts`'s loops), and a test of 5–6 woodland stands holds 1–2 GB.
- **The fixes, thresholds untouched:**
  - `tests/world.ts`'s `World` steps season by season and **fails when a world passes 1.5M live metamers** ("unregulated growth: N live metamers at season s"): one failed assertion instead of a poisoned module;
  - the evidence loops free each seed's world when they are done with it;
  - the 2D-era per-test timeouts (90 and 120 s) are gone. The backlog's config allows 30 minutes, because a 3D `forest` season costs ≈ 0.2 s natively and more in WASM.

The full backlog against this branch, with the top at 200 (`code/2026-10-31-pre-c1/evidence-2d-in-3d.md`): **39 of 78 pass, 39 fail, and none trap.**
- **The count:** the backlog holds 78 tests. I2's note said 90, with 47 passing, but its own table lists 43 that did not pass, so 35 passed then.
- **Four fail as unregulated growth:**
  - `shadow`'s two (M1);
  - R1 off (an ablation);
  - **the mixed wood at season 388** (1.6M metamers): its trees reach the 200 top too (item 5).
- **The other 35 miss thresholds,** as I2 listed: slenderness, floors, crown bases, the shade orders, whorls and spirals.
- **Running time:** the slowest tests take 2–5 minutes each in 3D, and the whole backlog about 4 hours on this VM with four workers.

### 3. The phone

Highest is the hedgerow, 219 ms in one lane and 197 in four; the interior is 145 and 130; the garden at 160 cells 163 and 127, at 66 cells 67 and 56. **One lane already meets the budget**, so D need not fall. ### 4. Threads
**WASM threads need nightly Rust.** The standard library has to be rebuilt with atomics:
- `-C target-feature=+atomics,+bulk-memory,+mutable-globals` and `-Z build-std=std,panic_abort` (nightly only);
- a shared memory (`--shared-memory --import-memory --max-memory=…`), whose maximum is reserved up front: a large maximum can fail on phones;
- wasm-bindgen's thread support and a pool of workers sharing that memory (or wasm-bindgen-rayon);
- `SharedArrayBuffer`, so a cross-origin-isolated page (COOP `same-origin`, COEP `require-corp` or `credentialless`): the host page must send them, and every resource it embeds must allow it.

The core is ready for it: `threads` gives the same bits on four threads natively (ADR 0025's fixed lanes). **Parked:** nothing needs it while one lane meets the phone's budget.

**Worker lanes are not a free production path.** I2's four-lane season (separate instances, no shared memory) timed the lanes' light only. The lanes also have to:
- receive the season's leaf area (W × top × D × 4 B each);
- send back their partial Q and V (16 B a cell below the top) to be summed in lane order;
- each hold a sweep of their own (≈ 20 B a cell): three more lanes are ≈ +125 MB at the garden's 160 cells, against item 1's budget.

The return trip alone, timed on the main side (the VM idle, medians of 9; `lane-return.txt`):

| Stand | Partial field a lane, MB | Copy out, 4 lanes, ms | Sum in lane order, ms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `forest`, season 40 (top ≈ 85) | 18 | 30 | 59 |
| `forest`, season 100 (top ≈ 125) | 27 | 68 | 51 |
| garden 160 | 19 | 19 | 31 |
| garden 66 | 8 | 10 | 14 |

That is 24–118 ms. Four lanes saved 50–60 ms of light on the VM (I2), so as built they gain nothing, before the leaf area's trip out is even counted. The sum would be faster in WASM SIMD than in this JavaScript loop; the copies would not. **Recommendation:** for I3 the production path is **one lane**. Lanes come back only with a smaller return (each lane samples its partial field at the buds and the snapshot's cells, not the whole field) and if a phone needs them after C1.

### 5. The world's top
**The top binds, and a crown pinned at it runs away.**
- **`forest` at season 150:** pinned at 131 under a 130 top, and at 132–139 under 200. It was identical to season 120.
- **The garden's planting at 160 cells under a 130 top:**
  - the birches reach the top at season 80, the beech and hornbeam at 120;
  - the beech then doubles every 15–20 seasons: 8k metamers at season 100, 51k at 160, **293k at 200**;
  - 265k of them lie in the top ten rows, nearly all born in the last 30 seasons;
  - the whole garden goes from 27k to 305k metamers, and with O4's recruits to 1.3M.
- **The mechanism:** nothing can grow above the top to shade it. The top row sees the open sky however dense it grows, so its buds keep winning, and shoots that cross the top get no light and cast no shade.
- **Under a 200 top** the same planting stays regulated: 24–30k metamers to season 200.
- **But height does not level off in 3D:** the beech reaches 202 at season 200 and the hornbeam 199–202 from season 140. The 2D model's plateau (90–96 cells, R1's L_h) does not carry over.
- **The mixed wood (230 cells, the flora test) runs away at season 388** under the 200 top, as the 130 top's did by 200.

A higher top postpones the runaway. C1 and C3 must bring the trees to a plateau below it (the C1 note finds R5's own-shade signal is most of the missing plateau).

**What it costs:**
- **Light: nothing** until a tree grows into the headroom. The sweep marches only up to the highest leafy row: `forest`'s light at seasons 41–50 and 91–100 is the same at both tops, within the VM's noise (155–171 and 225–248 ms). At seasons 141–150 it is 289–310 against 254–261, because the trees there are taller.
- **Memory:** W × D × 24 B a row at birth, ≈ 80 B at peak: +70 rows is +17 MB at the garden's 160 cells at birth (51 against 33 MB).
- **Opening the slab's top instead** (sky above H) is cheaper in memory, but leaves above H would cast no shade on each other, which is the runaway itself.

**Done:**
- the woods' top is 200 (`TOP` in `presets.ts`): the ring (`forest`, `mixedWood`, `succession`, `thinning`, `clearing`) and the five single-species woods;
- the goldens are unchanged, since nothing reaches 130 in their 10–20 seasons;
- the garden's own `WORLD_HEIGHT` (130, which sets the plate's width) is unchanged. Its "world" frame fits at least half the top, now 100 cells where it was 65, so a young garden is drawn smaller. Framing is I3's.

## What would have stopped this
- **For the top:** if raising it had changed the goldens or the light's cost before trees used the headroom, it would have been a calibration change, not a fix. Neither happened.
- **For the harness:** if the trap had come from freed memory (the GC theory), the guard would not have helped. Every trap here followed a world passing 1.5M metamers or a test holding its seeds' worlds.

## Conclusion
- **Canvas 2D works for 3D.** The preview's blank canvas was the preview's own bug.
- **Memory is about 1 MB a cell of plate width.** The proposed rule (256 MB) holds for a phone and a 160-cell laptop plate, not for the garden's widest plates.
- **The backlog runs.** Its failures are now the model's: `shadow` and R1 off do not regulate in 3D.
- **One lane meets the phone's budget.** Threads (nightly) and worker lanes (their return trip) are parked.
- **The top was a hidden runaway.** It is raised to 200, and height's plateau joins C1's targets.
