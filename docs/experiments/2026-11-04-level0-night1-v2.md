# Level 0, night 1 against targets v2: beech, oak, birch (2026-11-04)

The post-sweep session for night 1 (the plan's §k): the gate, the gallery and `shapeFlag`, the loss contributions, the
bound report for oak and birch, and beech's between-seed spread. Targets, T and the gate are v2's as registered
([note](2026-11-03-level0-targets.md), 7615b01); nothing in them changed. No core change, no params accepted.

Files: `docs/experiments/fits/level0/` holds the fits (`S.json`, `S-best.json`, `S-progress.csv`), the gate tables
(`S.gate.txt`, `.json`), the gallery output (`S.gallery.txt`), beech's spread (`beech.spread.txt`) and the bound probes
(`bounds-*.txt`, `.json`). Scripts: `level0_fit_gate.sh` (the night), `m27_gallery.py --fit … --seeds 6`,
`level0_spread.py`, `level0_bounds.py`, `level0_bounds_probe.py`.

## Verdict
**No species passes Level 0 on v2.** Beech comes nearest: 9 of 20 seeds, up from 2 of 20 on v1.

Height is no longer what blocks. Two things do:
- **Oak: crown radius.** Every seed is over T at 60.
- **Birch and oak, underneath: the girth trajectory.** DBH barely grows after 60 while height keeps rising, so no
  single setting fits 60, 100 and 150 together. This is ADR 0050's "smaller, real trunk effect", now the binding one
  for birch.

**No fit is flagged by the gallery.** Beech's gallery check cannot separate the hand form from round 2's spindle, so
the panel decides for beech.

## The bound report: oak and birch
Under the bound rule, this is a report, not more generations. Part 1 is the search's own 228 candidates per species:
Spearman associations, which are not causes (`level0_bounds.py`). Part 2 is a probe: the fit with one knob moved, past
its bound where asked, on seeds 1–5 (`level0_bounds_probe.py`). The bounds are lifted for the probe only.

## Recorded, not fixed: height at 100 is scored three times (asked)
Three scored terms read the same measurement, the stand's mean height at season 100:
- `stand.lateShare` = height100 / height60 (`analysis/treesim/fit.py`, `seed_traits`);
- `stand.height100` (v2's time anchor);
- `stand.talloH.1` (the median z of each measured tree's height at 100 against its DBH).

The allometry's height equals the stand metric's to 0.01 cell (the v2 note's check), so they are one measurement.
lateShare also reads height at 60, which `stand.height` scores. For beech, oak and pine (whose specs score all three),
height at 100 therefore carries three terms' weight; for birch, two (its lateShare is reported). Not changed: targets and weights are as
registered. In tonight's fits the three agree (each contributes ≤ 0.51), so the triple weight did not trade against
another term here.
