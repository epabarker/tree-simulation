# What keeps the oak's upper laterals from spreading into its flank? (exploratory)

_2026-11-08. Wayfinder ticket [#90](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/90), map [#73](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/73). It follows [Does a water-status cap on internode length round the open-grown oak and maple?](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/89) ([note](2026-11-08-turgor-length.md)). The code is `wayfinder/turgor-length` 41d8c68 plus a new probe. **Exploratory: seeds 1–3, lone trees in the 60 m open-grown box, 101 seasons.** No adoption claim, and no rule or parameter was changed in the core._

## Answer

**Apical control through C3's λ is what keeps the flank empty. The angle does not, and R2's relay, acrotony and vigour drop do not.**

- **The oak's upper half is one axis.** At season 100, the path from the root to the oak's highest node changes axis 0–5 times (HEAD 1–5, fric50 0–1). Maple's changes 51–58 times. Above about 0.4 H the oak is a single monopodial axis: the trunk, or one level-1 limb that took over from it.
- **C3 passes about 1% of that axis's resource to its laterals.** On the upper leader, laterals hold 1.2% of the light at a node (median, seasons 30–100) and get 0.7–1.5% of v_in. On maple's upper leader, laterals hold 75–97% of the light and get 80–98% of v_in. By half height only 6–22% of the oak's v_base is left in the leader. Lower limbs hold 85–91% of the oak's leaf.
- **The buds are lit but get too little resource.** Lateral buds on the oak's upper leader have light 0.39–0.48 of Q_top (median). Their median v is 0.02 at HEAD and 0.08 with the length cap (seasons 60–100), against R4's v_cont of 1. Only 0.4% (HEAD) and 5% (fric50) reach their threshold. They end the season as leafy short shoots (62–88%) or resting dormant buds (8–27%). 4–8% grow, and R4 aborts none.
- **The few that grow point the right way.** New shoots leave at 43–46° from vertical, and branch roots sit at 47–56°. The level-1 drift to 85° (`agedSetPoint`) has no time to act: a branch born on the upper half after season 20 lives a median 6–10 seasons and reaches 1–2 metamers. 26–43% of these branches are shed, almost all by R1's carbon test (P = 0, one metamer, light 0.43–0.53 of Q_top). These are the leafless twigs from the flat-cap ticket. C5's light test sheds none of them. The ones that survive stay near the trunk (90th-percentile reach 5.4 m against a crown about 35 m wide). 64% of their new metamers fall inside the top cone and 0.1% in the flank shell (HEAD).
- **Acrotony and the vigour drop are not the cause.** The oak has no vigour drop (`vigourShare` unset), and R5's neighbour shade is 0 on a lone tree. Acrotony (κ 0.75) raises the effective λ from 0.52 to 0.61 at HEAD. That cuts the lateral share by a factor of about 0.7, while the gap to maple is a factor of about 80.
- **R2's relay is not maple's difference.** Maple with `relay` 0 still changes axis 46–55 times and puts 1.9–6.1% of its new growth in the flank (HEAD 3.6–10.4%).
- **λ is the difference.** After R2's ontogenetic fall, maple's λ is below 0.5 on every level (0.48 / 0.43 / 0.42), so a lateral gets more resource per unit of light than the continuation. The oak's λ stays above 0.5 (0.52 / 0.52 / 0.46). Maple given the oak's λ schedule (`lamoak`: levels' λ, aC, dLambda and ramp) changes axis only 8–36 times. On its upper leader the laterals' light share falls from 0.97 to 0.05–0.08, it grows to 29–35 m against 25–27 m, and its W/H is 0.44–0.67 against 0.83–0.86. It becomes a tall, narrow tree with one main axis.

**Why the oak's top stays empty:** with λ > 0.5 every lateral on the oak's single top axis gets about its own light's share of a resource that the lower limbs have mostly taken already. That is ≈ 0.02–0.08 against a threshold of 1, so the top never divides. The flank is far from the trunk because the lower limbs make the crown wide. To reach it, upper laterals would need to grow 8–12 m, but they reach 5 m at most. A sink limit cannot help, because no lateral there grows enough to take up carbon. This is the "loss of apical control" that [How do real crowns become rounded?](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/74) ranked third. In this model it is the gate for the oak's flank.

## Smallest change to test next

**Make the oak's λ fall below 0.5 with age.** This is a parameter change only: `r2.dLambda` 0.04 → 0.08–0.12, with `aC` kept at 13.5 or moved later (≈ 30) so the young oak stays excurrent. For comparison, maple's fall is 0.078 from aC 7. Run it with and without `turgorLength` at s* 0.5, on oak seeds 1–3 in the 60 m box. Diagnostics: axis switches on the leader path, the laterals' light share on the upper half, flank share of new growth (≥ 5%), top-fifth leaf, W/H and height. Birch should be checked as the contrast (it should stay excurrent).

**Caveat from `lamoak`:** moving maple's λ above 0.5 changed its width and height more than its flank share (4.9–8.5%). The flank is measured relative to the crown's own width, so a narrow crown's flank sits near the trunk. For the oak, which is already wide, falling λ should put growth where the flank is, but whether it rounds the outline or splits the top into a flat table (the ADR 0031 risk) is what the test has to show.

## Method

- **Probe** `rust/core/examples/flank_probe.rs`: replays the season phase by phase as `cap_probe.rs` does, and reproduces #89's season tables exactly (height and metamer count, every season, checked on `head-oak-s1` and `fric50-maple-s2`). Each season, after allocation, it records each leader node's v_in, the split between its main and lateral slots in v and in q, its nominal λ (`lambda_at`) and the λ that reproduces the split, its order and pa, and the slot it enters by. It records each live lateral bud on the leader (state, age, q ÷ Q_top, v, R4's threshold) and its fate after growth: grew (metamers, length, angle), refused by `c1.budBreak`, rest as a short shoot, rest dormant, or abort. It records each lateral branch on the leader (size, leaf, light, P/M, deficit run, angle, the centroid and reach of its tips, buds passing threshold, growth, and shed cause from cap_probe's rule). Every 10 seasons it dumps buds and new metamers tagged by origin (upper lateral, lower lateral, leader, other).
- **Runs** `runs/oak-flank/` (git-ignored): `head` and `fric50` (#89's params) for oak and maple, plus two maple ablations, `norelay` (`relay` 0) and `lamoak` (the oak's λ rows, aC, dLambda and ramp). 18 runs, about 6 min on 12 workers.
- **Analysis** `code/2026-11-08-oak-flank/summary.py`. The crown geometry and flank (shell ρ ≥ 0.7, 30–60° from vertical) are as in the turgor notes.

## Limits

Three seeds, lone trees only, and seasons ≤ 100. The oak's own λ test has not been run. The "leader" is the path to the highest node, which can run along a limb that took over from the trunk. That is the axis the flank question is about, but the analysis does not track the trunk's own continuation below it.
