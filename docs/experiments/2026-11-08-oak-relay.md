# Does treating a takeover as a relay let the open-grown oak divide its top while keeping its spire and low limbs? (exploratory)

_2026-11-08. Wayfinder ticket [#94](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/94), map [#73](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/73). It follows [Which open-grown oak does the rounded-crown page take…](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/93) and the [oak-schedule note](2026-11-08-oak-schedule.md). **Exploratory: seeds 1–3, the lone oak in the 60 m open-grown box, 101 seasons, no length cap.** No adoption claim._

## Answer

**Fail, so by the ticket the rounded-crown page takes the early dome (`d12a13`).**

1. **The fact: the oak's takeovers are not losses.** At HEAD, every takeover onto the pa‑1 leader at seasons 30, 60 and 100 (3 seeds) is a lateral that outgrew a live main axis. In the season the lateral was born, its host's main bud was active and grew 1–3 metamers, and the main axis is still there, 0.2–18 m below the lateral's branch top. No main bud had died, been shed or gone dormant. Over HEAD and the slow fall `a13r40` (6 runs), 260 of the 315 takeovers on the leader path at 30, 60 and 100 were "main active and grew that season". The rest were laterals that broke later on a node already carrying its main child (39), main buds that rested as short shoots while the lateral grew (9), or laterals born beside a short-shoot main bud that grew the same season (7). **A relay on loss never fires for the oak.**
2. **A vigour trigger does not separate the leader from the limbs either.**
   - *At birth:* the leader-taking laterals got 0.78–0.91 of their main bud's resource and grew as many metamers.
   - *Later in their lives:* comparing the resource flowing into the lateral against the main continuation, two of the three young takeovers at HEAD never overtook it (maximum ratio 0.91–0.93). Meanwhile 5–13 of the 17–37 big limbs per tree did: the oak's wide low limbs carry enough leaf to out-draw the trunk above them.
   - *Height does separate them:* a lateral on a low trunk node never tops the main continuation, which carries the tree's top.
3. **The patch: the overtop relay** (`r2.relayOvertop`, off by default, goldens unchanged). At the start of growth, a lateral whose branch reaches above its host's main branch takes the host axis's level by ADR 0033's substitution: order − 1 over its subtree, metamers and buds. It only promotes. It works as intended: in `Rk101` the upper leader has pa 0's falling λ (0.44), not the limbs' kept 0.56.
4. **But it misses the bars, and it takes the young form away.** At 30 every relay condition is a rounded bush, not HEAD's spire, even under HEAD's own λ (`R`). Once the leader leaves the trunk at a node, the trunk above it stalls, and the laterals along the stalled stretch top it and stand up as pa 0 one after another. The top never divides by the ticket's measure (≤ 19 switches without the early fall) and the flank never reaches 5% (0.0–2.9%, against 4.9–7.3% for the λ schedules without the relay). The relay makes each new leader vertical (pa 0's set point 0), as ADR 0033 found with beech. Holding the arm's birth angle (`…A`: `forkAt` set too high to fire, which turns on ADR 0040's arm set point) restores the low reach. It does not restore the spire or the flank.

- **Against the pass (all on ≥ 2 of 3 seeds):** no condition divides the top with ≥ 30 switches and ≥ 5% flank while keeping height at 30. The two that divide (`Rd12a13`, `Rd12a13A`) are the early fall's short young oaks (15.7–18.4 m at 30). The miss is not on low reach alone, so it does not go back to the user under the ticket's rule.
- **What the pictures add** ([seed 1](figures/2026-11-08-oak-relay-s1.png), [seed 2](figures/2026-11-08-oak-relay-s2.png), [seed 3](figures/2026-11-08-oak-relay-s3.png)): by 100, `Rk101` and `Rk100` are full, rounded domes on seeds 1 and 3 (`Rk101` seed 2 is narrow, W/H 0.64). The flank measure misses them because the crown is already full, so new growth lands inside it. But they are domes reached through a bushy youth, the early dome's trade by another route.
- **No-harm checks fail too.** With the relay on, beech grows taller (36.8–39.1 m at 100 against 33.7–36.2) and narrower at its base (low half-width 14.5–18.0 m against 19.8–23.9). Birch keeps its height but its top divides (17–24 switches against 3–7). A general overtop relay is not neutral for other species.

## What this settles, and what it leaves

- **The oak's leader and limbs can be put on different levels** (the relay does it), **but not without losing the young spire**, because the same rule promotes the laterals along a stalled trunk. A rule limited to the tree's single leader would avoid the cascade, but it would no longer be local (the leader is the path to the tree's highest tip), and it is not specified here.
- **The rounded-crown page takes the early dome (`d12a13`)**, as the ticket's fail branch says. The relay stays a probe patch on this branch.
- **The flank measure fails on a crown that is already full** (`Rk101` at 100). A full dome may legitimately put its new growth inside the crown; w90 plus the eye is the better read, as #92 proposed.

## Method

- **Step 1 probe** `rust/core/examples/takeover_probe.rs` (new). It replays the season phase by phase. Before growth it notes every metamer's main slot (child, bud and state, or lost). After growth it stamps each new lateral shoot with that, its resource against the main bud's, and what the main slot did. Every season it tracks each lateral's resource flow in against its host's main child. Every 10 seasons it writes the takeovers on the leader path (`takeovers.csv`), the laterals at birth (`births.csv`) and every lateral on a pa‑0 host (`limbs.csv`). Runs: HEAD and `a13r40`, seeds 1–3, `runs/oak-relay/fact/` (git-ignored).
- **The patch** `r2::relay_overtopped` (`rust/core/src/principles/r2.rs`), called at the start of `season::phase_grow` when `r2.relayOvertop` is true, with its unit test `a_lateral_that_tops_its_hosts_main_branch_takes_the_hosts_level`.
- **Runs** `code/2026-11-08-oak-relay/conditions.py` writes the params from #91's open-box base (`runs/oak-lambda/base`; beech is the oak's file with the species swapped). The `…A` files add `forkAt` 1e9 to `Rk101` and `Rd12a13`. #90's `flank_probe`, 101 seasons, dumps every 10, 12 workers, `runs/oak-relay/out/`.
- **Analysis** `code/2026-11-08-oak-relay/summary.py <dir> <species>` is #92's script with the condition list and a species argument (output in `summary.txt`, `summary-birch.txt`, `summary-beech.txt`). `figure.py` draws the contact sheets.

## Limits

Three seeds, one lone tree, seasons ≤ 100. One form of the trigger (strict height, read once a season, promote only). The arm angle was borrowed from ADR 0040's fork arms, not designed for relays. No garden run.
