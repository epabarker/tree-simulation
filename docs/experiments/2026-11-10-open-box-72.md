# The open-grown box at 72 m: no beech or oak bud leaves it in 20 seeds

_2026-11-10. Issue [#103](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/103), [ADR 0062](../decisions/0062-light-entry.md) decision 2. It follows build (a)'s [note](2026-11-09-open-box-build.md) (the 60 m box) and #104's [note](2026-11-10-ground-rest-light.md) (wider crowns). Code: `code/2026-11-10-open-box-72/reach.py`, with build (a)'s `run.sh`._

## What changed
- `OPEN_BOX` is 288 cells (72 m square; it was 240, 60 m). `TOP` stays 200 cells (50 m).
- `oakOpen`, `beechOpen`, `openLoneParams` and the feature probe's lone stage follow it, with the tree at the centre (144, 144).
- The box is sized for beech and oak over 100 seasons. Birch's runaway leaders and limbs are a defect of their own ([#106](https://github.com/epabarker/tree-simulation/issues/106)), and the box is not sized for them.

## Method
- Lone open-grown beech and oak, seeds 1–20, 100 seasons, natively, 12 at a time: `BOX=<cells> TOP=<cells> run.sh <out> 1..20 beech,oak` on a tree that has #104's fix.
- Two boxes: 80 m × 65 m (320, 260), to measure the reach uncensored, then 72 m × 50 m (288, 200), the box as built.
- **Reach** is the farthest metamer end from the root along x or z at season 100: what a square box must hold. `metamers.csv` is written at season 100 only, so a limb that reached further earlier and was shed shows only in `budsOutside`.
- The pre-#104 runs of the 80 m box (build (a)'s) are the paired baseline.

## Result
| season 100, seeds 1–20 | beech | oak |
|---|---|---|
| Zero buds outside in every season, 72 m box | **20 of 20** | **20 of 20** |
| Reach, 72 m box: min–max (median) | 25.8–31.4 m (28.0); farthest s14 | 20.0–31.4 m (25.7); farthest s15 |
| Spare to the 36 m half-side | 4.6 m | 4.6 m |
| Tallest in any season | 36.8 m | 34.7 m |
| Farthest reach in the 80 m box, before #104 → after | 31.6 → 31.4 m | 32.8 → 31.4 m |
| Paired change in reach from #104, 80 m box: median (range) | +0.5 m (−4.6 to +3.1) | +0.1 m (−5.2 to +4.3) |

- **The acceptance check passes:** 40 of 40 trees keep every live bud inside the box in every season. In the 60 m box after #104 it held at season 100 in 18 of 20 beech and 19 of 20 oak.
- **#104 did not raise the farthest reach.** Thirteen of 20 crowns reach further in each species, but the maxima did not grow.
- **The 72 m and 80 m boxes grow the same crowns.** Every seed's reach agrees between them to within 0.1 m, although the run hashes differ (the tree stands at a different cell).
- **The 50 m top is enough:** 13 m clear of the tallest beech.
- **Crowns are still widening at season 100.** In seven of the eight widest trees (80 m runs) the spread peaks in seasons 99–100. The box is sized for 100 seasons.

## Cost
| seed 1, 100 seasons | s/season, seasons 91–100 (season + snapshot) | 100 seasons | memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| `beechOpen`, WASM, 72 m | 0.85 + 0.08 | 62 s | 934 MB (749 MB at 60 m) |
| `oakOpen`, WASM, 72 m | 0.84 + 0.04 | 63 s | 733 MB (546 MB at 60 m) |
| native, 12 at once, 72 m | | beech 419–731 s, oak 249–757 s | beech 884–993 MB, oak 643–696 MB |

- Memory is up by about a quarter for beech and a third for oak. Time per season is inside build (a)'s range (0.79–1.44 s for beech), which laptop heat varied.
- **The lab runs it.** `?preset=beechOpen&seed=1&seasons=100` in headless Firefox (Canvas 2D) reached season 100 in 86 s with no page error. A phone was not tried.
