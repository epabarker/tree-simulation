## 0. The review's corrections, checked

### 0.1 R1's sapwood cost is linear in tips — right, and exact
`r1.rs:66–86`: each metamer pays m_w·π·r_live²·l with r_live = (Σ_tips r0ⁿ)^(1/n); at n = 2 (every preset and species) the tree pays m_w·π·r0²·Σ(path length to each live tip). C6's `radius` is never read (`heartwood_costs_nothing`, r1.rs:194, sets it to 1 and expects the same cost).

**Run:** lone oak (`oakOpen`), R3 and O2 off, 60 seasons, seeds 1–3, base against r0 ÷ 8 and m_w × 64 (species patch, see 0.6). Production, maintenance, height, metamer and bud counts **bit-identical** in every season; trunk radius ÷ 8.000; H/D 15–18 → 124–146. The rescale is exact when k is a power of two (binary scaling); at k = 2 with r0 0.011295 the last bit differed and the chaotic trees had parted by season 2–6. **Use k = 8** (r0 0.02 → 0.0025, m_w 1 → 64; species r0 ÷ 8).

### 0.2 The sky's top band is 72.5–90° — right
`slab_sweep.rs:80`: sin e_i = lo + (1 − lo)(i + f)/K, lo = sin 15°. Band 15 of 16 spans 72.5°–90° (static f = 0.5: 77.6°), ≈ 9.7% of the sky's weight at p = 1; the lowest band 15.0–17.8°. Round 2's "none at the zenith" was wrong.

### 0.3 The woods run the fitted beech — right
`flora.ts:354` builds `FLORA` from `FITTED_FLORA`; `presets.ts:97` plants it. A 1-season `mixedWood` run records beech r1 `Lh 108.9 hyperbolic, mf 0.07697`, r6 `qMin 0.01257`, c6 `r0 0.02477`; hornbeam `Lh 92.4, qMin 0.01255`; oak `Lh 41.02, qMin 0.1134`. Round 2's "m_f 0.035, L_h 60" is `HAND_FLORA`'s.

### 0.4 H/D is not C6's alone: with R3 on, the rescale does nothing to the trunk
`forest`, 150 seasons, seeds 1–3, base against k = 7 (r0 0.002857, m_w 49):

| | metamers/seed | median H/D | trunk r | trunk's own stress wood | buds/tree | P/tree | P per bud | crown width | height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base s150 | 121k | 11.8 | 3.4–3.9 | 0% | 4266 | 2042 | 0.48 | 21.8 | 81.4 |
| k7 s150 | 62k | 15.2 | 2.7–2.8 | 3% | 2320 | 875 | 0.38 | 19.9 | 84.7 |

The lone oak with R3 on at k = 8: trunk 3.90 against base 3.82.

**Why:** the trunk's pipe sum is scars. Split at the root into its childless metamers (C6's tips, r0² each) and the rest: tips 0.4–1.2 of 9–15 at r0 0.02 (scars and memory 92–96%), and 0.006 of 6.7–7.4 at k7 (100%). `c5.rs:91` records a shed branch's scar as its **full** radius, stress wood included; `c6.rs:34` passes children's pipe part only (radius − stress wood) but scars go in whole. With thin pipes every twig is over σ_target as soon as it bears a leaf (r_target ≈ 0.0075 cells for one leaf unit at half a cell), so every twig thickens to the mechanical radius in its first season (`r3.rs:139`), and when shed, that radius enters the pipe sums. The trunk becomes Σ shed branches' mechanical areas, independent of r0; reaching H/D 70 that way would need σ_target ≈ 90× (unphysical). Spec §R3 says stress wood is "local to the stressed metamer (not passed on in the pipe sums)": the scar carrying it is an inconsistency, not a design.

**R3 ablations at k = 8** (`forest`, 100 seasons, 3 seeds; base 133k metamers, H/D 13.3):
- k8: 74k, H/D 14.4, trunk 2.55;
- sag off (φ 0): 85k, H/D 20.2, trunk 1.96: sag is part of the loss, not most of it;
- posture off: 78k, height 52, crown width 50: the trees flop; posture is essential at these radii;
- stress-wood girth off (girthGain 0): every tree snaps in season 1 (σ > σ_break with pure pipes at r0 0.0025).

**The fix, tested** (a scratch build with `c5.rs:91` recording `radius − stress_wood`, not committed; `forest`, 150 seasons, seeds 1–3):

| | metamers/seed | alive of 90 | height | H/D | trunk r (its stress wood) | crown width | floor | stand LAI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base, scar fix, s150 | 122k | 68 | 81.4 | 11.9 | 3.44 (0) | 22.3 | 16.4% | 9.5 |
| k8, scar fix, s100 | 78k | 70 | 65.0 | 65.5 | 0.47 | 28.7 | 12.4% | 7.4 |
| k8, scar fix, s150 | 56k | 62 | 67.1 | **65.6** | 0.43–0.59 (0.05–0.17) | 27.7 | 14.9% | 6.0 |

- At r0 0.02 the fix changes almost nothing (no stress wood there), so 2D-era behaviour is kept.
- At k8 the stand's H/D lands in 60–80 with no further knob. The trunk is still 97% scars, but now pipe-sized ones.
- **Also moved:** the plateau falls from 81 to 67; 6 more planted trees die by 150; crowns widen from 22 to 28 (wider than the 21-cell spacing, so gaps close better: 29% against 36%); metamers halve. The light now falls steadily down the crown (0.62 at row 60, 0.31 at 40) instead of a step at the canopy's top.

So at realistic tip radii mechanics *must* set the twig radii, and the tree it grows has half the buds at the same light and production per bud: crown LAI 6 (under crowns 11) against the base's 9 (15). Spec §10's crown LAI is 2–8: the rescaled tree is nearer it, and costs half the metamers. The picture changes (sparser crowns, thin twigs): the user has to see it.

### 0.5 The floor is not leaf area: it is the faces and the gaps
Base s150: light by row 1.00 at row 90, 0.27 at 70, 0.21 at 60, then **flat at 0.17 from row 50 to the ground**; columns under LAI 20+ read 0.160, gaps (LAI < 1, 35% of the ground) 0.171. Stand LAI is 9.4 (14.5 under crowns), where Beer–Lambert from above would give < 1%; k7 dropped LAI to 6.2 and the floor stayed at 0.169. The review's −ln(floor)/k_ext ≈ 3.5 assumed a homogeneous canopy.

**τ = 0 control** (`faces [0, 0]`, 3 seeds): floor **6.3% at season 100, 9.1% at 150** against 14.0% / 16.4% at τ 0.2. The faces (ADR 0024's "wood interior" look) supply about half the floor and light the lower crown the same way. Inside an opaque-faced wood the 2–10% target is met at 100–150; it then rises as trees die (76 → 68 of 90 alive from 100 to 150; gaps 27% → 35% at τ 0.2, 32% → 41% at τ 0) and the survivors do not widen into the holes (crown width 22 at a 21-cell spacing throughout). Crown cover, not LAI, is the long-run limiter, and the crown base cannot lift while the faces light rows 0–60 at 17%.

### 0.6 A trap for every run: species trees ignore world patches
`oakOpen`'s tree is `species: "oak"`, so a `--params` patch of `c6`, `r1`, `r3` … does nothing to it (the species' own block wins); it needs `--species-patch`. The first invariance run "passed" with identical radii for that reason. `forest` plants no species, so world patches apply there. Every script in this plan says which it uses.
