# I3, the 3D renderer: the plan

Track 2 of the [review of 2026-11-01](../reviews/2026-11-01-3d-port-review-2.md) (§5). Written 2026-10-30 (the machine's clock) on `3d-port` 44e6657, after form steps 1–2 (ADR 0027). The planning measurements are in [`code/2026-10-30-i3-plan/`](../experiments/code/2026-10-30-i3-plan/) (`sizes.ts`, `picture.ts`, `bench.mjs`, their outputs). They are one seed on this VM: sizing, not evidence.

## 0. What the review claimed, checked
All six renderer claims hold at 44e6657:
- `snapshot.rs:113–114` projects each metamer to an in-plane `angle` and `length`; `z0` and the frame columns ride beside them.
- Only WebGL2 draws wood far to near (`scene.ts:869` `drawOrder`, used at `webgl2.ts:632`). Leaves are laid out per snapshot in metamer order (`scene.ts:1096`) and drawn after all wood, in both renderers (`webgl2.ts:591–598`, `canvas2d.ts:141–151`). Canvas 2D batches wood by colour and width, not depth (`canvas2d.ts:196–247`).
- `plateWidth` (`garden.ts:253`) has no cap; `forestParams` clamps to 54–520 (`presets.ts:506`).
- `WORLD_HEIGHT = 130` (`garden.ts:244`) against the woods' `TOP = 200`; the "world" frame's floor is half the world's height (`scene.ts:547`), so a young garden is framed for 100 cells.
- Nothing under `src/` reads `dirX/Y/Z` or `refX/Y/Z`.
- `cli/first-paint.ts:48` has `FIRST_PAINT_3D = false`: the manifest lists no widths and the garden grows from seed.

Also found:
- **The intro self-paces.** `request()` (`garden.ts:490`) asks for one season ahead; a slower worker only stretches the intro, it never drops frames. No adaptive season pace is needed.
- **ADR 0008's pseudo-depth code** (`MIN_SPREAD`, `project`, `projectAll`, `axisZ`, the two-layer clusters) is dead on both pages but kept alive by `scene.test.ts`, `seasons.test.ts` and `scene.bench.ts`.
- **Every plate holds 300 trees.** `o4.maxTrees` is 300 whatever the plate's area, so a 66-cell phone plate carries a seedling every 14 cells² of ground (≈ 0.9 m²), and its metamer count is mostly recruits.

## 1. Measurements

### 1.1 A 3D checkpoint is 16 MB
`treesim --preset mixedWood --seed 10 --checkpoint-at 40` (230 cells, 300 trees): **15.9 MB gzipped** at season 40 (64k metamers, 70k buds; 250 B a metamer), 18.5 MB at 100. The checkpoint holds every f64 column of the tables (`checkpoint.rs`), which gzip barely touches. ADR 0014's budget was 300 kB a checkpoint; the review's Q7 guessed 1–6 MB. **A shipped checkpoint is out** for every plate.

### 1.2 The garden's planting by plate width
`forestParams(width, 10)`, one lane, WASM under Node on this VM (within ≈ 10% of the user's phone and laptop single-threaded, G1), dead trees discarded as the worker does (`sizes.ts`):

| Width | Season | Trees | Metamers | Snapshot | Snapshot ms | Season ms (mean) | Memory | Cover, upper half | Leaves per covered cell |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 66 | 40 | 300 | 20.3k | 1.9 MB (95 B/m) | 17 | 79 | 55 MB | 0.22 | 3.9 |
| 66 | 100 | 300 | 24.7k | 2.3 MB | 27 | 134 | 68 MB | 0.24 | 9.0 |
| 160 | 40 | 300 | 45.8k | 4.2 MB (93 B/m) | 90 | 222 | 134 MB | 0.20 | 2.6 |
| 160 | 100 | 300 | 52.0k | 4.7 MB | 144 | 300 | 161 MB | 0.26 | 6.3 |
| 200 | 40 | 300 | 60.1k | 5.4 MB (92 B/m) | 148 | 249 | 175 MB | 0.19 | 3.1 |
| 200 | 100 | 300 | 59.9k | 5.3 MB | 74 | 396 | 225 MB | 0.18 | 4.3 |

- **Season time** at 160 cells is 222–300 ms in one lane: at the 250 ms intro budget, not under it. The intro only stretches (§0), so this is pace, not breakage. The 66-cell phone plate is comfortable.
- **The snapshot costs 17–148 ms** on the worker, on top of the season. It is built once in Rust and copied out, so the time is in what it computes: `tree_metrics` for 300 trees each season (`snapshot.rs:210`), including `ground_light` over each tree's x–z extent (`metrics.rs:117`). To be profiled in step 3.
- **Memory** at 200 cells reaches 225 MB by season 100, against ADR 0026's 256 MB for the whole worker; the specimen beside it added ≈ 90 MB in the pre-C1 note. The cap belongs below 200.
- **Cover.** The share of 1-cell picture cells in the canopy's upper half holding a leaf (a metamer ≤ 5 seasons old) is 0.18–0.26, with 2.6–9 leaves per covered cell. The 2D garden measured 3–7% (τ note §3). That is the mixed wood's green wall: four times the covered cells, each holding several leaves drawn one over another.

### 1.3 A first-paint picture
What a quantised picture of the season-40 snapshot would weigh (`picture.ts`: base position to 1/16 cell, angle, length, radius, order, age and light as 8- or 16-bit columns, parents and the leafy buds as deltas, gzipped), against the raw snapshot and the checkpoint:

| Width | Metamers | Picture, gzipped | Snapshot, gzipped | Checkpoint, gzipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 66 | 20.3k | **225 kB** | 1.2 MB | 4.1 MB |
| 160 | 45.8k | **500 kB** | 2.7 MB | 9.5 MB |
| 220 | 66.7k | **730 kB** | 3.9 MB | 13.9 MB |

A picture is 20× lighter than a checkpoint, and still 2–4× ADR 0014's 300 kB on the laptop's plates. It can only be a first frame: the worker regrows the same seed from scratch behind it and takes over, which the core's determinism makes seamless (the regrown season 40 is the picture, bit for bit).

### 1.4 Frames in headless Chromium
`bench.mjs`: the page's `?bench` through the 40-season intro, software GL (SwiftShader), device pixel ratio 2, on this 4-core VM (`bench.txt`). The draw times are relative, not a phone's; the pattern is what matters.

| Case | Metamers at 40 | Draw p50 / p95, ms | Frame p95, ms | Long frames | Worker a season, mean / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garden, phone viewport (390 × 844) | 16.9k | 0.8 / **48** | 100 | 28 | 99 / 170 |
| Garden, laptop viewport (1440 × 900) | 44.1k | 1.3 / **124** | 100 | 13 | 257 / 520 |
| Lab, `mixedWood`, WebGL2 | 60.2k | 1.7 / **93** | 100 | 27 | 333 / 626 |
| Lab, `mixedWood`, Canvas 2D | 60k | did not finish 40 seasons in 10 minutes | | | |
| Lab, `forest`, WebGL2 | 113k | 2.3 / **135** | 100 | 24 | 407 / 668 |

- **A frame is cheap; a season is not.** Draw p50 is 1–2 ms, so the tween's per-frame CPU work is fine at 60k metamers. The p95 of 48–135 ms is the frame that takes a new snapshot: the sort by z (`drawOrder`, a JavaScript comparator sort of 17–113k indices), the leaf layout, the colour indices and the geometry rebuild, all on the main thread. That is one dropped-frame hitch every season, which at the intro's 4 seasons a second is a stutter. M15 moved this work from per frame to per snapshot for 2k metamers; at 50k it needs moving off the main thread or out of JavaScript.
- **Canvas 2D does not keep up** with a 60k-metamer stand at this size. It stays the fallback for devices without WebGL2 and for the loupe's culled drawing, at the phone's plate, where it has been seen to work (pre-C1 note §0).
- **The worker** under this bench is 99–407 ms a season, with the bench's own software GL competing for the four cores; §1.2's figures (79–249 ms, Node alone) are the cleaner ones.

## 2. Decisions
Put to the user on 2026-10-30 with the numbers above; **all four decided as recommended** (ordered slices; the live intro with a picture only for reduced motion; recruits scaled by area; the plate capped at 160).

1. **Depth order: ordered slices, not a depth buffer.** The snapshot's wood and leaves are drawn far to near in z-slices (64 of one cell for WebGL2, 8 for Canvas 2D), wood then leaves within a slice, each slice a range of the existing instance batches. It keeps the alpha-blended, antialiased SDF edges as they are (a depth buffer would leave sky-coloured halos where a near branch's soft edge wrote depth over a leaf behind it), gives Canvas 2D the same picture at its coarser step, and costs 2 × slices draw calls a copy of the ring. The alternative, a depth buffer with alpha-tested edges, was declined for the halos and for losing the antialiasing.
2. **First paint: the live intro, nothing shipped; a picture only for reduced motion.** The intro self-paces, so slow devices stretch it rather than drop frames; the checkpoint (4–14 MB) is out; the garden's time-lapse was M23's choice and stays. Reduced-motion visitors today wait while `growStill` steps 40 seasons (3–10 s). The picture (225 kB on a phone's plate) is the remedy if that wait reads as broken; decide after the phone check, with 300 kB the budget and the laptop plates served by their nearest width at or under it.
3. **The plate cap: 160 cells** in `plateWidth`, not the 200 ADR 0026 sketched. A 200-cell plate reaches 225 MB by season 100 on its own; with the specimen beside it the worker passes 256 MB. 160 cells is 161 MB by season 100, and the site's wide plate (about 220) loses little: the trees are framed by height, not width.
4. **Recruits per area.** `forestParams` scales `o4.maxTrees` with the plate's ground area (300 on the ring's 210 × 64, so about 95 on a 66-cell plate), rather than 300 on every plate. The phone's plate today carries a seedling every 14 cells² of ground, which is both the metamer count and the carpet under the trees. A preset change, not a principle change; C5 recalibrates the stand's dynamics on it.
5. **Foliage drawing.** Leaves stay one card per leafy bud at the species' true scale, drawn in depth order, and nothing is thinned to hide the model (ADR 0024). The coverage instrument (§1.2's cells and leaves per cell, plus pixel coverage of the canopy read back from the canvas in `?bench`) reports before and after; the user judges on the phone against form's stands. Only leaf opacity and the minimum on-screen leaf size are I3's knobs.
6. **The snapshot:** drop `refX/Y/Z` (nothing will read them); keep `dirX/Y/Z` for the leaf's foreshortening in depth (a leaf on a shoot pointing at the viewer draws short). Move the z-order sort into the snapshot (Rust, a permutation column) so the main thread does not sort. Profile `tree_metrics` for 300 trees a season and give the garden a cheaper cadence if it is the snapshot's cost.

## 3. Steps
Branch `claude/i3` off `3d-port`, PR into it. `npm run check` after each step; the goldens never change (the renderer and the snapshot's order column are outside the hashed tables; check with `npm run bench -- --compare`). Reads and headless checks here; the look on the phone is the user's, from the PR's branch (`npm run dev -- --host`).

### Step 1: the picture in depth
- `rust/core/src/snapshot.rs`: a `zOrder` i32 column, the metamers' indices far to near (sort by z0 descending, index ascending; `sort_unstable_by` on a Vec of indices), after `prevIndex`; drop the three `ref*` columns. `src/core/types.ts` (`Snapshot`, the column schema), `src/wasm/world.ts` (the carve-out), `docs/architecture.md`'s snapshot section. `scene.ts` `drawOrder` reads the column (keep the JavaScript sort for tests' hand-built snapshots without one).
- `scene.ts`: `layoutLeaves` walks metamers in z order, so the leaf list is far to near with no second sort; a `sliceStarts` table per snapshot for wood and for leaves (64 slices over [0, D)); remove ADR 0008's pseudo-depth (`depth`, `MIN_SPREAD`, `project`, `projectAll`, `axisZ`, `BACK_SHADE`, the two-layer clusters and `Leaves.back`), and its tests and the bench's `depth: true`.
- `webgl2.ts`: per copy of the ring, for each slice far to near: `drawBatch(segments, range)`, `drawBatch(leaves, range)`; buds and ghosts as before. `drawBatch` takes a first index and count. `canvas2d.ts`: 8 slices, the counting sort by (colour, width) within each.
- Leaf foreshortening: a leaf's drawn length × hypot(dirX, dirY) of its shoot, width unchanged, so shoots toward the viewer carry short leaves (the picture's own cue for depth, no darkening: ADR 0024's view).
- Tests: `scene.test.ts`: a hand-built snapshot with a near and a far metamer at one (x, y): the near one draws later; leaves of the far one draw before the near one's wood. `canvas2d.test.ts`: the same through the recording context. `structure.test.ts` or a new `snapshot.test.ts`: `zOrder` is a permutation, descending in z0.
- **Expected:** draw p50 unchanged within 1 ms; the per-season hitch (§1.4's p95) falls by the sort's share (measure before and after with `bench.mjs`); the mixed wood's near crowns cover its far ones.
- **Stop if:** the slices cost more than 4 ms a frame on this VM at 60k metamers (then fewer slices, 16, before anything else), or the ring's three copies × 128 draw calls show as the frame's cost (then merge the copies into one instance stream).
- **Visual:** the lab's `mixedWood` and `forest` at 150, `oakOpen`; the garden; the user on the phone.

### Step 2: the plate, the frame and the recruits
- `garden.ts`: `PLATE_HEIGHT` (130, the plate's intended height) replaces `WORLD_HEIGHT` in `plateWidth`, with `PLATE_MAX = 160` as the cap; `scene.ts`'s "world" frame takes its floor from a `frameHeight` the garden passes (0.5 × PLATE_HEIGHT = 65 cells), not from `world.height`. `forestParams` scales `o4.maxTrees` by ground area (W × D over 210 × 64, times 300, at least 20). `presets.ts`'s doc; `node cli/export-presets.ts` is unaffected (`forestParams` is not a preset) but `mixedWood`'s own `maxTrees` stays 300.
- Tests: `plateWidth` caps at 160 for any aspect; a 66-cell plate's `maxTrees`; the frame of a young garden under a 200 top is 65 cells, not 100 (a `scene.test.ts` fit check).
- `sizes.ts` again at 66, 160: metamers, memory, season time with recruits per area. **Expected:** the phone's plate under 10k metamers at 40, the laptop's under 30k; memory unchanged (the field dominates).
- **Stop if:** the user finds the garden too bare of seedlings (then a higher per-area number, decided by eye, not by cost).

### Step 3: the season's cost on the worker
- Profile the snapshot (`snapshotMs` is already reported): `tree_metrics` × 300 with `ground_light` over each tree's x–z extent. If it is most of the 17–148 ms, the garden asks for metrics every 4th season (`SnapshotOptions.metrics: "every" | "lean"`, the lean one keeping `tree`, `alive`, `height`, `species`, `sown` for the page's anchors and taps), and the lab keeps every season.
- Re-measure §1.2's table. **Expected:** snapshot under 20 ms at 160 cells.
- **Stop if:** the cost is in the copy-out, not the metrics (then the column list is the lever: `light`, `resource`, `stress` only when the lab's colour mode asks).

### Step 4: foliage in depth, judged
- The coverage instrument: `?bench` reports, at the end of the intro, the share of canopy pixels covered by leaves (read back from the canvas, green against the sky and wood) and §1.2's cell measures; `bench.mjs` prints them. The note records the garden at 66 and 160 before step 1 and after each step.
- Knobs, and only these: leaf opacity (0.92 today, per `fillLeaves`), `LEAF_MIN_PX`, the species' `length` only where M23's true scale was wrong in 3D (a leaf's length is in cells, unchanged by the rescale). No thinning by light, age or count.
- The user's judgement on the phone against form's `forest` and the mixed wood at 150: the canopy reads as crowns in depth, not a wall; trunks and the lower crown show through where the model has them bare.
- **Stop if:** the picture only reads right with leaves thinned (then the model carries too much foliage, which is form's §4 / C5's question, and I3 does not hide it).

### Step 5: reduced motion and first paint
- Time `growStill` on the phone's plate (the user: `garden.html` with reduced motion on, the console's `seasonMs`). If the wait is over about 3 s, build the picture: `cli/first-paint.ts` writes `first/<width>-<seed>.pic.gz` for widths ≤ 100 (`FIRST_PAINT_3D` becomes the picture path), a reader in `garden.ts` draws it as the first snapshot while the worker regrows the seed and takes over at season 40; the 300 kB budget gates it.
- ADR 0028, "3D first paint: the live intro; a picture for reduced motion", recording §1.1 and §1.3 and the decision.
- **Stop if:** the regrown season 40 differs from the picture (then the snapshot's quantisation, or a non-determinism, and the picture waits).

### Step 6: the phone, and wrap-up
- The user runs `garden.html?bench&seasons=40` on the phone and laptop from a preview deploy (the user's; `preview-3d` has the old `i2.ts`) or over the LAN; the numbers go in the note beside §1.4's, with the 250 ms season and the frame p95 at 60 Hz (no long frames through the tween) as the budget. Memory from `wasmMemoryBytes` logged under `?bench`.
- Docs: the note `docs/experiments/2026-10-30-i3.md`; ADR 0028; ROADMAP's port entry; STATUS; the housekeeping items the review's §6 lists that this touched (`WORLD_HEIGHT`, the `GardenOptions` doc, `wasmMemoryBytes`, ADR 0008's code). `/wrap-up`, PR into `3d-port`.
- **Stop if:** the phone's season is over 250 ms at the phone's plate (then recruits per area or the plate's share, before any lane work), or frame p95 shows long frames through the tween after step 1 (then the remaining per-season work moves to the worker: the leaf layout as columns in the snapshot).

## 4. Sessions
Steps 1–2 in one session (the picture in depth is the one the user has to see); steps 3–4 in a second; steps 5–6 in a third, after the phone numbers. Form's step 5 (20 seeds) can run beside any of them; the species re-fit changes the metamer counts again, so step 6's budget table is re-read then.
