# Species in 3D (track 3): the plan

Track 3 of the [review of 2026-11-01](../reviews/2026-11-01-3d-port-review-2.md) (§5): the ten species re-fitted on the 3D core. Written 2026-10-30 (the machine's clock) on `3d-port` 422d704, after form steps 1–5 (ADR 0027, the 20-seed evidence) and the I3 plan. The planning measurements are in [`code/2026-10-30-species-plan/`](../experiments/code/2026-10-30-species-plan/) (`trials-3d.ts`, `trials-3d.txt`): one seed on this VM, sizing, not evidence.

## 0. What the fit is, and what 3D broke
**The harness** (ADR 0020, 0023): CMA-ES over 18 dotted-path parameters of a species' patch, in a unit cube with log scales, against literature targets in `analysis/fit/targets/<species>.toml` (each with its source and band); the loss Σ w·((seed-mean − target)/band)² plus death penalties (`analysis/treesim/fit.py:197`). The trials are `cli/trials.ts`: a lone tree on 160 × 160 read at seasons 30 and 60; a row of ten trees 21 cells apart on 230 × 160, the interior six read at 60 and 100; the shade house (a roof of eight densities over a seedling, alive at 30); release, held out. A long-lived WASM evaluator per core (`cli/fit-eval.ts`) answers the Python driver. The full budget is 5 restarts × 100 generations × 12 candidates × 5 seeds, up to 30k tree-runs; round 2's beech took 640 s for 1164 candidates × 4 seeds on the user's 12 cores, **about 1.65 core-seconds a (candidate, seed)** in 2D. `analysis/experiments/m27_adopt.py` writes `src/core/flora-fitted.ts` from the fits plus `hand-overrides.toml`; `m27_holdout.py` runs the hold-out (survival order, the mixed wood over 400 seasons, allometry, release).

**What 3D changed, found by reading the harness against the port:**
1. **Every fitted value is a 2D fit.** The species' R1 (L_h, m_f), r0, Q_ss, leaf area, T_rel, R3's c and R2's rows were fitted under planar crowns and the 2D sky. ADR 0027 rescaled r0 and m_w for all of them and hand-set σ_break; nothing else moved. The fitted beech, hornbeam and oak run to the 200 top (form note; STATUS).
2. **The trial worlds are 2D-sized.** `loneParams` and `standParams` set a world of height 160 (`cli/trials.ts:41, 47`); `mergeParams` keeps `oakOpen`'s depth 64 and faces [0.2, 0.2] underneath. Beech reaches 151 cells by season 100 in its wood: the stand trial at 100 runs into a 160 top, and a pinned crown runs away (pre-C1 note §5). **Both trials take the woods' top, 200.**
3. **The stand trial cannot measure a floor.** It is one row of ten between faces at τ 0.2. Round 1 showed τ 0.2 caps the dark end at 6–8% under any canopy, and a one-row stand between opaque faces (τ 0) lights its floor through the empty depth beside the row. Beech's `stand.floor` target is 1.5% ± 0.8, hornbeam's and holly's similar: unreachable in that trial, so the fit would bend other parameters chasing it. **Floor leaves the loss** and is read in the hold-out, in each species' own wood planted per area (`beechWood` and the others, `perArea`, at τ 0, where form's 20-seed floor was read).
4. **`stand.crownRatio` is the branch measure** that cannot lift (form step 2): it reads 0.99 whatever the crown does. **It becomes `stand.leafCrownRatio`** (`metrics.rs`, form step 2's `leafCrownRatio`), targets unchanged (beech 0.55, birch 0.38, described, half weight); form's generic stand read 0.58–0.64 in a stand at τ 0.2, so the bands are reachable.
5. **`flora-fitted.ts` no longer matches its generator.** `flora.ts`'s `in3d` applies each species' phyllotaxis and E = 4·10⁶ at runtime over `FITTED_FLORA` (ADR 0025), and ADR 0027's r0, m_w and σ_break were hand-edited into the file (`m27_adopt.py` carries the same rule, `GIRTH_SCALE`, `SIGMA_BREAK`). Regenerating today writes the overlay's values into the file as well, which is harmless (the overlay is idempotent) but makes the diff unreadable. **The overlay moves into `m27_adopt.py`** (its `PHYLLOTAXIS` map and E), `in3d` goes, and the regenerated file must reproduce `rust/presets.json` byte for byte before any fit lands (the goldens prove it).
6. **The shade house's roofs** (`ROOFS`, "≈ 61% to 2% of the open sky under K = 7") were set for the 2D sky. The trial measures the light under each roof, so `survivalLight`'s interpolation still holds, but the range must still bracket 2–30%: checked in §1.
7. **Cost.** A 3D tree-run is tens of seconds, not 0.6 s (§1). The fit's shape follows from that number.

- **Cost: 30–55 s a (candidate, seed)** for lone + stand, plus 4 s for the shade house's eight roofs where it runs (birch, oak, beech, hornbeam). Against 2D's 1.65 core-seconds that is **20–33×**. The full budget (30k tree-runs) would take 30 hours a species on 12 cores; the quick budget (4800) 5 hours. The stand trial is two thirds to four fifths of the cost.
- **What is off in 3D, and what is not.** The scale-free shapes mostly hold: early share within a band for eight species, late share for six, W/H for seven, branch angles (not shown) close. The misses are the ones ADR 0027 and the review predicted: **heights** 1.1–1.6× their targets (beech's lone tree 143 at 60, against a stand target of 84; fir's stand 160 at 100, the trial's top); **slenderness** 50–132 against 48 and 70 (every species' r0 is its 2D fit ÷ 8, which put the generic stand at 70; the species need their own); **crown ratio** 0.92–0.99 (the branch measure, §0.4); **floor** 0.10–0.29 (the one-row trial, §0.3). So the fit's job in 3D is height, girth and shade, with form mostly carried over.
- **The shade house** at roof 3.3 gives 15.2% light for every species (the roof is in the light model, not the tree); the 2D sweep gave less. `ROOFS` must be re-measured so the eight roofs still bracket 2–30% (step 1).
- **The top binds:** fir's stand at 100 reads 160, the trial's top. The trials take the woods' top (200) before anything is fitted.

## 2. Decisions (put to the user with these numbers)
1. **The fit's shape: a two-stage re-fit, warm-started from the round-2 fits, not the full budget.** Stage A fits the nine parameters 3D moved most (height, girth and shade: `r1.Lh`, `r1.mf`, `c6.r0`, `r6.qMin`, `r6.leafArea`, `c5.Trel`, `r3.c`, `r2.hJ`, `c3.alpha`) with R2's rows held at their round-2 values; stage B fits R2's nine form parameters (λ by level, set-points, branch angle, η, `aC`, `dLambda`, `kappa`) with stage A's held. Each stage: one restart from the warm start (σ 0.15), population 9–10, 3 seeds a generation, 35 and 20 generations with the existing patience rule. About 1500 tree-runs a species, **≈ 1.5–2 hours on 12 cores**; the eight garden species in one night. The alternatives: the full 18 parameters warm-started at population 12 for 50 generations (1800 runs, but CMA-ES in 18 dimensions with 12 a generation converges poorly; round 2 used 100); the quick budget as it stands (5 hours a species).
2. **Floor leaves the loss** (§0.3) and is read in the hold-out, in each species' own wood planted per area at τ 0 (`<sp>Wood` with `perArea`, 150 seasons, 3 seeds, the floor at 100 as form's evidence reads it), against the same targets. The alternative, planting the stand trial per area so the floor can enter the loss, triples the dearest trial (30 trees), about 4 hours a species.
3. **The garden's eight species first** (`MIXED`: birch, oak, willow, beech, hornbeam, rowan, holly, maple), then fir and pine for the lab. The shippable bar is the mixed wood.
4. **Adoption stays the user's** (ADR 0020): the fits are reported with the hold-out, and `flora-fitted.ts` changes only on the user's word, as a new ADR. Beech, hornbeam and oak levelling off below the top in the mixed wood is the bar.

## 3. Steps
Branch `claude/species-3d` off `3d-port`; PR into it. The sessions prepare and read; the fits run on the user's machine overnight (`uv run --project analysis python analysis/experiments/m27_fit.py …`, Node 24 on PATH, the WASM built). Goldens change only at adoption (step 5), with the reason in the devlog.

### Step 0: the generator reproduces the file
- `m27_adopt.py` takes over `flora.ts`'s `in3d`: the `PHYLLOTAXIS` map and E = 4·10⁶ applied to each patch after the fit's assignments and the overrides; `in3d` and the map leave `flora.ts` (`FLORA = FITTED_FLORA`). Regenerate; `node cli/export-presets.ts`; **`rust/presets.json` must be byte-identical** (`git diff --exit-code rust/presets.json`) and `npm run check` green with no golden change.
- **Stop if:** `presets.json` differs (then the overlay and the file disagree somewhere; report the diff before touching either).

### Step 1: the trials in 3D
- `cli/trials.ts`: `loneParams` and `standParams` take `height: TOP` (200) from `presets.ts`; `runStand` reports `leafCrownRatio` as `crownRatio` (and the old measure as `boleCrownRatio` for the record); `ROOFS` re-measured by a small script (the light under each roof in the 3D shade house) and extended until the darkest is ≤ 2% and the brightest ≥ 30%, with the measured lights in the note.
- `analysis/fit/targets/*.toml`: `stand.floor` moves from `[[trait]]` to a new `[holdout]` key `floor` with the same target and band; `stand.crownRatio` keeps its name and targets (now the leaf measure; say so in the file's comment).
- `analysis/treesim/fit.py`: `FitSettings.budget("refit3d")` and a `stages` option (the two parameter sets by path), or two `--stage` runs; `--start round2` reads `docs/experiments/fits/round2/<sp>.json` (move today's results there; `fits/` holds round 3's).
- `m27_holdout.py`: a floor section (each species' wood per area at τ 0, 150 seasons, 3 seeds, floor at 100 against the target), and the mixed wood's 400 seasons at 3 seeds (not 6: a 3D run is minutes, not 3.5 s), reporting canopy trees per species every 50 seasons and the tallest tree against the top.
- A smoke run on this VM (`--smoke`, 4 workers): the pipeline end to end, ≈ 10 minutes. **Expected:** it completes and writes under `runs/`.
- **Stop if:** the smoke's candidate-seed cost is over 60 s on this VM (then shrink the stand trial to six trees, interior two, before the overnight run).

### Step 2: the overnight fits (the user's machine)
- The eight garden species, `--budget refit3d --start round2`, 12 workers; each species writes its file as it finishes (`--skip-done` resumes). Report per species: the loss before and after, the traits against targets, which parameters sit at a bound (ADR 0020's rule: a finding, not something to fix by tightening).
- **Expected:** heights and slenderness within a band for the measured targets; shapes kept within round 2's. **Stop if:** a species' best loss is above its round-2 loss (then the trial or a target is wrong for 3D, not the search).

### Step 3: the hold-out
- `m27_holdout.py` on the fits: survival order (Spearman over the ten, the six unfitted species at their 3D values), the floor in own woods, the mixed wood's succession and the tallest tree against the top over 400 seasons, allometry (Δ, the l–d slopes), release. The note with the tables; lab links for each species.
- **Stop if:** beech, hornbeam or oak still reach the top in the mixed wood (then R1's hyperbolic discount is the question, ADR 0021, which is the user's).

### Step 4: hand adjustments, if any
- As ADR 0023: overrides with reasons, measured with the hold-out, in `hand-overrides.toml` under a `[round3d]` section or per species with a date.

### Step 5: adopt and wrap up
- The user's decision, recorded as ADR 0028 "Species re-fitted in 3D". Regenerate `flora-fitted.ts`; `node cli/export-presets.ts`; `npm run golden:update` (the woods and `mixedWood` change; reason in the devlog); `npm run check`; the garden's mixed wood at 300 seasons, 3 seeds, in the lab and on the user's phone. Note, ROADMAP (the species rows), STATUS, devlog. `/wrap-up`, PR into `3d-port`.
- Then fir and pine the same way, for the lab.

## 4. Sessions
Steps 0–1 in one session (with the smoke run), then the overnight fits; steps 3–5 in a second session, reading the results. I3's steps 3–6 can run between them; the mixed wood's look in depth (I3 step 4) and the species' heights (this track) are judged together on the phone.
