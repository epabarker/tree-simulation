The planning measurements are in [`code/2026-10-30-species-plan/`](../experiments/code/2026-10-30-species-plan/) (`trials-3d.ts`, `trials-3d.txt`): one seed on this VM, sizing, not evidence.

## 0. What the fit is, and what 3D broke
**The harness** (ADR 0020, 0023): CMA-ES over 18 dotted-path parameters of a species' patch, in a unit cube with log scales, against literature targets in `analysis/fit/targets/<species>.toml` (each with its source and band); the loss Σ w·((seed-mean − target)/band)² plus death penalties (`analysis/treesim/fit.py:197`). The trials are `cli/trials.ts`: a lone tree on 160 × 160 read at seasons 30 and 60; a row of ten trees 21 cells apart on 230 × 160, the interior six read at 60 and 100; the shade house (a roof of eight densities over a seedling, alive at 30); release, held out. A long-lived WASM evaluator per core (`cli/fit-eval.ts`) answers the Python driver. The full budget is 5 restarts × 100 generations × 12 candidates × 5 seeds, up to 30k tree-runs; round 2's beech took 640 s for 1164 candidates × 4 seeds on the user's 12 cores, **about 1.65 core-seconds a (candidate, seed)** in 2D. `analysis/experiments/m27_adopt.py` writes `src/core/flora-fitted.ts` from the fits plus `hand-overrides.toml`; `m27_holdout.py` runs the hold-out (survival order, the mixed wood over 400 seasons, allometry, release).

**What 3D changed, found by reading the harness against the port:**
1. **Every fitted value is a 2D fit.** The species' R1 (L_h, m_f), r0, Q_ss, leaf area, T_rel, R3's c and R2's rows were fitted under planar crowns and the 2D sky. ADR 0027 rescaled r0 and m_w for all of them and hand-set σ_break; nothing else moved. The fitted beech, hornbeam and oak run to the 200 top (form note; STATUS).
2. **The trial worlds are 2D-sized.** `loneParams` and `standParams` set a world of height 160 (`cli/trials.ts:41, 47`); `mergeParams` keeps `oakOpen`'s depth 64 and faces [0.2, 0.2] underneath. Beech reaches 151 cells by season 100 in its wood: the stand trial at 100 runs into a 160 top, and a pinned crown runs away (pre-C1 note §5). **Both trials take the woods' top, 200.**
3. **The stand trial cannot measure a floor.** It is one row of ten between faces at τ 0.2. Round 1 showed τ 0.2 caps the dark end at 6–8% under any canopy, and a one-row stand between opaque faces (τ 0) lights its floor through the empty depth beside the row. Beech's `stand.floor` target is 1.5% ± 0.8, hornbeam's and holly's similar: unreachable in that trial, so the fit would bend other parameters chasing it. **Floor leaves the loss** and is read in the hold-out, in each species' own wood planted per area (`beechWood` and the others, `perArea`, at τ 0, where form's 20-seed floor was read).
4. **`stand.crownRatio` is the branch measure** that cannot lift (form step 2): it reads 0.99 whatever the crown does. **It becomes `stand.leafCrownRatio`** (`metrics.rs`, form step 2's `leafCrownRatio`), targets unchanged (beech 0.55, birch 0.38, described, half weight); form's generic stand read 0.58–0.64 in a stand at τ 0.2, so the bands are reachable.
5. **`flora-fitted.ts` no longer matches its generator.** `flora.ts`'s `in3d` applies each species' phyllotaxis and E = 4·10⁶ at runtime over `FITTED_FLORA` (ADR 0025), and ADR 0027's r0, m_w and σ_break were hand-edited into the file (`m27_adopt.py` carries the same rule, `GIRTH_SCALE`, `SIGMA_BREAK`). Regenerating today writes the overlay's values into the file as well, which is harmless (the overlay is idempotent) but makes the diff unreadable. **The overlay moves into `m27_adopt.py`** (its `PHYLLOTAXIS` map and E), `in3d` goes, and the regenerated file must reproduce `rust/presets.json` byte for byte before any fit lands (the goldens prove it).
6. **The shade house's roofs** (`ROOFS`, "≈ 61% to 2% of the open sky under K = 7") were set for the 2D sky. The trial measures the light under each roof, so `survivalLight`'s interpolation still holds, but the range must still bracket 2–30%: checked in §1.
7. **Cost.** A 3D tree-run is tens of seconds, not 0.6 s (§1). The fit's shape follows from that number.
8. **The stand trial was a hedgerow** (found by the first beech pilot, [note](../experiments/2026-10-30-species-3d.md); the user, 2026-10-30). One row between τ 0.2 faces is lit from the faces and the empty depth beside it, so it grows edge trees: squat, with late growth unchecked. No r0 gave it H/D 70 while the lone tree stayed at 48. The evidence's H/D 70 was read in a closed stand. **The stand trial is now closed:** three rows of four on an 84-cell periodic ring, spacing 21, faces [0, 0], the woods' top; the middle row's four trees are measured at 60 and 100 (height, H/D, `leafCrownRatio`, late share). It costs about twelve trees: ≈ 6 s to season 100 on the user's laptop, against 45 s for the hedgerow. The one-row stand stays as the release trial's (`rowParams`, held out).
9. **Form metrics read 3D crowns** (the user, 2026-10-30): the crown profile (`widestAt`, `crownFullness`) and `lone.spread` take each band's larger extent of x and z, and `forkHeight` counts a lateral only if it also climbs 0.7 of the trunk's climb above the node (`form.rs`). See the note for the beech targets whose reading changes.

## 2. Decisions
The user paused the fit's shape on 2026-10-30 and put §0–1 to an outside reviewer (someone who knows computation, not this code). Their answer, and what the code says to it:

**The search is least squares, not black-box.** The loss is a weighted sum of squares over 13–15 named residuals, the start (the round-2 fit) is near the solution, and the residuals are nearly smooth in the parameters. CMA-ES folds 14 informative numbers into one scalar an evaluation; Levenberg–Marquardt uses all 14, and from a near point needs 5–12 iterations of (centre + one perturbation a parameter) × seeds, each iteration perfectly parallel. Budget: a screening Jacobian (≈ 35 evaluations) + 10 iterations (≈ 30 each) + verification ≈ 4 core-hours a species, **≈ 3 hours on 12 cores for the eight**, against ≈ 14 for the two-stage CMA-ES, which stays as the fallback. **Adopted**, with two adjustments the reviewer could not see:
1. **Tip radius is not downstream.** The reviewer proposed solving r0 in closed form from a stored run. r0 enters R1 (maintenance m_w·π·r0²·path) and R3 (thin pipes make stress wood; slenderness feeds windthrow). ADR 0027's invariance makes it nearly so: the search carries r0 with **m_w derived as 64·(0.0025/r0)²**, so R1's cost does not move with it and only mechanics couples. r0 then takes a **closed-form update each iteration** from the measured slenderness (trunk radius is r0·√tips plus 10–30% stress wood, form note), not a Jacobian column.
2. **The shade residual is a step** (alive or dead per roof at 30, interpolated). The core tracks reserve and starvation, so `runShade` reports **seasons survived under each roof, capped at 30**, and `survivalLight` interpolates that continuous margin's crossing. The discrete statistic stays reported.

**Revised after the first beech pilot (the user, 2026-10-30):** LM does the screening and the first descent as built, then **CMA-ES from the LM point finishes** (initial covariance from (JᵀJ)⁻¹, population 10, 4 seeds, 30 generations, patience 15). The **active set is a noise test**: a parameter is active if it moves some residual by more than that residual's seed noise at the centre. **Acceptance is the measured targets within their bands on 6 fresh seeds**; the loss is a progress indicator, and the comparison with round 2's 2D loss is dropped. The closed stand runs to 100 throughout. Stop rules: CMA-ES's best not improving for 15 generations with measured targets still outside their bands, or a form metric that cannot be made 3D-aware without a spec question.

**Decided (the user: no preference; the plan's recommendations stand):**
3. **Floor leaves the loss** (§0.3) into the hold-out: each species' own wood planted per area at τ 0 (`<sp>Wood`, 150 seasons, 3 seeds, the floor at 100 as form's evidence reads it), the same targets. Planting the stand trial per area would triple the dearest trial.
4. **The garden's eight species first** (`MIXED`: birch, oak, willow, beech, hornbeam, rowan, holly, maple); fir and pine for the lab after.
5. **Adoption stays the user's** (ADR 0020): fits are reported with the hold-out; `flora-fitted.ts` changes on the user's word, as a new ADR. Beech, hornbeam and oak levelling off below the top in the mixed wood is the bar.

**The method, as it will be built** (`analysis/treesim/fit_lm.py`, beside `fit.py`):
- **Residuals:** r_i = √w_i·(s_i − t_i)/b_i for every trait in the targets file (the in-band form traits stay in the vector, so the growth moves cannot silently break them), a dead-tree residual (lone and stand, continuous: the share dead), and a Tikhonov term λ(θ − θ₂D) in the unit cube (λ small, 0.1) so the solver prefers the smallest move from the round-2 point.
- **Seeds:** fixed for the whole fit (common random numbers; 3 for the lone tree and shade, 2 for the stand, whose ten trees average its noise); fresh seeds for verification only.
- **Screening:** central differences on every parameter at the round-2 point, step 0.08 in the unit cube (a secant over the distance to be moved, not a tiny step: shedding and death are discrete), one seed, the stand to 60. The 14 × n matrix gives the active set (the parameters that move the off-target residuals), the collinear pairs (L_h against Q_ss on shade traits: LM's damping handles them), and the first step. Expected active set: `r1.Lh`, `r1.mf`, `r6.qMin`, `r6.leafArea`, `c5.Trel`, `r3.c`, `r2.hJ`, `c3.alpha`, plus r0 by its own rule; R2's rows held unless the Jacobian says otherwise.
- **Iterations:** `scipy.optimize.least_squares` (`trf`, literature bounds, `diff_step`), with `jac` a callable that evaluates the perturbations through `Pool.map` in parallel (the library's own differencing is sequential). Accept a step only if the full loss drops; halve otherwise. The stand to 60 until the step is small, then to 100 for the last two iterations (`stand.lateShare` needs 100, and it is the trait that catches the runaway).
- **Verification:** the finalist at full fidelity on 6 fresh seeds; report each trait's margin to its band edge, aiming at the band's centre.
- **Transfer:** the first two species' shift in log-parameter space becomes the other six's starting point.
- **Fallback:** two-stage CMA-ES as drafted (§2 of the previous draft), with the initial covariance from (JᵀJ)⁻¹, for any species where LM steps keep being rejected; plan B a 100-point quadratic response surface on the active box.

## 3. Steps
Branch `claude/species-3d` off `3d-port`; PR into it. Goldens change only at adoption (step 5), with the reason in the devlog. The pilot species runs on this VM in the session; the eight run on the user's machine (Node 24 on PATH, the WASM built, `uv run --project analysis …`).

### Step 0: the generator reproduces the file
- `m27_adopt.py` takes over `flora.ts`'s `in3d`: the `PHYLLOTAXIS` map and E = 4·10⁶ applied to each patch after the fit's assignments and the overrides; `in3d` and the map leave `flora.ts` (`FLORA = FITTED_FLORA`). Regenerate; `node cli/export-presets.ts`; **`rust/presets.json` must be byte-identical** (`git diff --exit-code rust/presets.json`) and `npm run check` green with no golden change. Move today's fits to `docs/experiments/fits/round2/` (`--start round2` reads there).
- **Stop if:** `presets.json` differs (report the diff before touching either side).

### Step 1: the trials in 3D
- `cli/trials.ts`: `loneParams` and `standParams` take `height: TOP` (200); `runStand(…, seasons)` takes 60 or 100 and reports `leafCrownRatio` as `crownRatio` (the branch measure as `boleCrownRatio`, for the record); `runShade` reports seasons survived (capped) beside `alive`; `ROOFS` re-measured in the 3D shade house and extended until the darkest is ≤ 2% and the brightest ≥ 30%, the lights in the note. `fit-eval.ts` passes `standSeasons` and the per-trial seed counts through.
- `analysis/fit/targets/*.toml`: `stand.floor` moves from `[[trait]]` to `[holdout] floor` (same target, band); `stand.crownRatio` keeps its name (now the leaf measure; say so in the comment); `shade.survivalLight` is computed from the continuous margin (`fit.py`'s `seed_traits`).
- `analysis/treesim/fit_lm.py`: the method above; `analysis/experiments/m27_fit.py --method lm` (CMA-ES stays the default for the record), writing the same results file plus the Jacobian and the per-iteration losses (`<sp>-lm.csv`).
- `m27_holdout.py`: the floor in own woods (per area, τ 0, 150 seasons, 3 seeds, at 100); the mixed wood's 400 seasons at 3 seeds (a 3D run is minutes), canopy trees per species every 50 seasons and the tallest tree against the top.
- **The pilot** (run on the user's 12-core laptop, 12 workers, not this VM; its timings and the stop it hit are in [the note](../experiments/2026-10-30-species-3d.md)): beech (the worst case: lone 143 at 60 against 84; stand 165 at 100). Screening ≈ 35 evaluations × 25 s / 4 cores ≈ 4 min; ten iterations ≈ 30 min; verification ≈ 5 min. **Expected:** heights and slenderness within a band, the form traits no worse than round 2's; the loss per iteration monotone after step halving. **Stop if:** more than three consecutive rejected steps (then the CMA-ES fallback for this species, and the Jacobian goes in the note), or the loss after ten iterations is above round 2's 2D loss.

### Step 2: the eight (the user's machine)
