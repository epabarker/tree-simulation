# The 0.17.0 box (2026-11-06): the garden's species at the right size and shape, on a stable base

_Opened after garden 0.16.0 shipped, when [the overseer's box](2026-11-05-overseer.md) closed. Revised the same day to fold in [the external review](../reviews/2026-11-06-external-review.md). STATUS carries the short form; this file holds the detail and the review's triage._

## Rules
- **Time box:** 2 sessions and 1 night, plus about half a session for the review's hygiene items (A0). It ends in a 0.17.0 release candidate or a recorded pause.
- **Gates are fixed before results and never loosened.**
- **G-forest is held against B0** (d2c4f53), not re-baselined.
  - Setup: `forestParams(160)`, 20 seeds, 300 seasons.
  - Pass: each count ≥ base − 2. B0 is floor 2, planted H/D 3, crown base 11, plateau 11.
  - Scripts: `experiments/code/2026-11-06-overnight/evidence/`.
- **Validation policy (from the review).**
  - Every measure is labelled **hard** (gates adoption), **soft** (reported, may block with a reason) or **advisory** (never evidence).
  - A failed instrument stays advisory: the sim-vs-sim judge is one.
  - The inverse of the garden rule: **no visual fix is called biological validation unless it passes an independent quantitative gate.**
- **No new mechanism** without a measured mismatch that existing params cannot fix. New mechanisms go behind a param, off by default, with an ADR. The review's "freeze" agrees with the existing rule; this states it.

## Birch so far (A1, 2026-11-06)
- **Cause:** birch puts its growth into the leader: trunk λ 0.588, about 400 buds on a 25 m recruit. The girth rule is right per leaf, storms are mild (about 1% windthrow a season), and the poles come from local seed, not seed rain.
- **Lever:** `r2.levels.0.lambda` (an existing param).

| | quarter cap | λ 0.53 | λ 0.55 |
|---|---|---|---|
| poles (≤ 120 in ≥ 15 of 20) | 13 | **18** | 17 |
| G-forest vs B0 (floor/H-D/crown/plateau) | 0/3/12/12 | 0/1/9/11 (pass, three at the limit) | 0/1/**6**/14 (fail: crown base) |
| lone height at 150 (band 33–42 m) | 48 m | 39 m | 40 m |
| lone W/H (real 0.52 [0.38–0.66]), soft | 0.30–0.36 | 0.63–0.68 | 0.51–0.56 |
| metamers at 200 | 0.94× | 1.13× | 0.97× |

- **Reading:** only 0.53 passes the hard gates, and only at their margins. Crown base isn't monotonic in λ (12, 9, 6 for the quarter cap, 0.53 and 0.55), so that check is noisy at this margin, as the review's brittleness point predicts.
- **Outcome:** λ 0.53 adopted. Leafier birch (twig laterals every node, every 2nd, or with λ 0.55 or 0.588) fails crown base in every variant (5–6). The likely cause is the one-tree lone comparator, which gets shallower and lowers the bar ([note](../experiments/2026-11-06-birch-poles.md)).
