## Why tonight's first item is beech
- **The garden's crowns stay low** because of its equal nine-species ring and a too-bright floor (14–18% of open light against a real wood's 2–10%).
- **A layered wood with a periodic depth** (ADR 0058) gives hornbeam and oak the real crown base medians (0.21) and a real floor (3.7–5.4%).
- **But its planted beech starves:** 22 of 24 die, as etiolated 4–10 m whips with 12–30 metamers, at about 3–5% light. Real beech saplings live for decades at 1–3%.
- **Beech is the garden's main species,** so the layered wood can't be adopted until beech survives shade.
