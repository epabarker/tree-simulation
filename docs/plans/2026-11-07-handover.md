**What the day and night found:**
- **Why the garden's crowns sit low** (about 5% of H, against a real stand's 22–38%):
  - an equal nine-species ring;
  - a floor that is too bright: 14–18% of open light, against a real wood's 2–10%.
  - It is neither C5 nor ADR 0055.
- **A layered wood** with a periodic depth (ADR 0058) gives hornbeam and oak real crown bases (median 0.21) and a real floor (3.7–5.4%). The [sheet](../experiments/figures/2026-11-07-layered-wood-sheet.jpg) reads as a wood interior.
- **But planted beech starves in it.** The cause is ADR 0044's spur lifespan: shaded saplings stop extending, and the wood-age clock strips every spur. With `r6.lifespan` off:
  - beech survives (27 of 40 against 6);
  - lone beech holds;
  - G-forest misses hd300 against B0 by one (0, needing ≥ 1).
- **Crown check v2** (per-species, five-seed lone comparators) passes every condition that v1 blocked.
- **Oak** (N3) loses its spire on fresh seeds with existing rules; it reads as a fan, not a dome.
- **The DAG** ([background/model-dag.md](../background/model-dag.md)):
  - `c5.T` is dead in every species;
  - holly's vigour-gate keys have no reader;
  - single-knob effects need ≥ 20 seeds to read.
- **Two defects:**
  - R1's deficit counts are not checkpointed, so `kShed` > 1 breaks save and restore;
  - B0 no longer matches the garden's config. B0 (d2c4f53) predates #50's recruit cap: it ran 229 live trees at 160 cells, HEAD 57, and composition counts every live tree, so hornbeam's share at 300 (0.43 → 0.13) almost certainly reflects the cap, not drift. G-forest has compared a 229-tree baseline with 57-tree runs since #50.

## 4. Rules that carry over

- **Choosing a knob** (the [model DAG](../background/model-dag.md)): before tuning any species or world param, check the DAG for dead levers (`dead_levers.png`, `reach.md`). Then read the ±10% sweep (`sweep_effects.csv`) for its likely sign and size against seed spread. Record both in that step's plan, and believe an effect only on ≥ 20 seeds.
- **Pushing:**
  - only the user merges, tags or pushes the site's `main` (which is production);
  - never merge `preview-3d`.
- **Gates:**
  - gates come before results;
  - hard, soft or advisory, per the validation policy;
  - no visual judge counts as validation.
- **Seeds:**
  - ≥ 20 for any claim;
  - fresh seeds for held-out tests.
- **Data:** commit summary statistics only, never per-tree tables from Weiser (CC-BY-SA) or FOR (GPL).
- **Compute:**
  - at most 12 simulation processes at once;
  - `/tmp` is a 16 GB tmpfs, so put bulky output in `runs/`;
  - one fresh process per memory measurement.
