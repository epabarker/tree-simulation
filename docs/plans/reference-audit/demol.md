# Demol et al. (Belgium destructive harvest) (audit, 2026-11-03)

- **Link:** https://zenodo.org/records/4557401 resolves (v1.0, 2021-02-23). **Licence CC-BY 4.0** (Zenodo API).
  The record cites Demol et al. 2021, *Trees* (doi 10.1007/s00468-020-02067-7); the 65-tree volume study is
  Demol, Calders, Verbeeck & Gielen 2021, *Annals of Botany* 128:805 (doi 10.1093/aob/mcab110).
- **Size:** 318 MB: `optimal_QSMs.zip` 15.8 MB (TreeQSM 2.3; file format inside not inspected),
  `pointclouds_clean.7z` 128 MB, `images.7z` 174 MB, `Destructive_and_qsm_data_DEMOL.csv` 17 kB (downloaded).
- **Regime:** forest; five monospecific, even-aged, thinned stands, 33–107 years (paper). TLS (VZ-400/1000).
  Leaf state: not documented.
- **Content:** per tree DBH, felled height, harvested fresh mass, wood density, QSM volume. No species column:
  the site code names it (FSYL, PSYLA/B, FEXC, LXDC: the record's species list).

|---|---|---|---|
| Fagus sylvatica | 15 | 25.5–38.5 | 18.8–24.5 |

No oak, birch, hornbeam, maple or the small species. **Use:** small and cheap (16 MB of QSMs), with felled
heights as ground truth: the converter's second gate after Wytham, for beech and pine. Too few for a
distribution (15 beech in one stand).
